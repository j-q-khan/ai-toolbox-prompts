# Full code: Gemini Image Generation: Nano Banana 2 in the Gemini App

Starter prompts for the Gemini app's Images page, written from Google's prompt tips (checked 2026-11-21). They were written from the docs, not run: your pictures will differ.

### 1. gemini-starter-prompts.md
```
# Starter image prompts for the Gemini app (Nano Banana 2 on the free version)
# Facts checked 2026-11-21 on Google's own help pages. Nothing here was run.
# Where to paste them: on a computer, gemini.google.com, Open Sidebar (top left), Images,
# then the text box at the bottom, then Submit.
# Google's three tips: start with create, generate or draw; name the style;
# describe what's in the picture, what it's doing, and the background or setting.
# Google's formula, from its overview page: [Create/generate an image of] [subject] [action] [scene]
# Swap the words in [square brackets] for your own. Keep real people and real brands out.

## 1. A first picture
Create a watercolor painting of a blue vase holding three white flowers on a sunny windowsill, with a pale green wall behind it, aspect ratio 2:3.

## 2. A card
Create a cartoon illustration of a smiling teacup with steam shaped like a heart, sitting on a saucer on a yellow tablecloth, aspect ratio 3:2.

## 3. A poster with words
Generate a flat poster design of a small red house under a big round tree, with the words "Garden Party, Saturday" in large clear letters across the top, aspect ratio 2:3.

## 4. A pet portrait, made up
Draw a charcoal drawing of a fluffy grey cat curled up asleep on a woollen blanket, soft window light from the left, plain background, aspect ratio 1:1.

## 5. Your own
[Create / Generate / Draw] a [style: watercolor painting / cartoon illustration / charcoal drawing] of [what's in it] [what it's doing] [the background or setting], aspect ratio [2:3 tall / 3:2 wide / 1:1 square].

## Follow-up edits (type these in the same chat, straight after the picture)
# Google's image page says you can change the background, replace an object, or add an element.
# Editing isn't available to users under 18 (Google's help page).
Make the vase blue and keep everything else the same.
Change the background to a starry night sky and keep everything else the same.
Add a small yellow bird on the windowsill and keep everything else the same.
Make the words on the poster bigger and keep everything else the same.

## Before you save or share
# Download on a computer: hover over the picture, Download full size.
# Google adds an invisible SynthID watermark and a visible watermark.
# Check a picture: Add files (below the text box), Upload Files, then ask "Was this image created or edited by Google AI?"
# Starting a new chat for a new picture saves usage (Google's limits page).

# Sources: support.google.com/gemini/answer/14286560 ; gemini.google/overview/image-generation ;
# support.google.com/gemini/answer/16722517 ; support.google.com/gemini/answer/16275805
```
