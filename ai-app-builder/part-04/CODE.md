### 2. base44-publish-and-phone-checklist.md
```
# Your first Base44 app with logins, from one prompt to a base44.app link and the phone stores (checklist, facts checked 2026-11-24)

To keep a copy: paste this into Notepad and save it with the box called Save as type set to All files, as base44-publish-and-phone-checklist.md. On a Mac: open TextEdit, choose Format, then Make Plain Text, before you paste and save.

Every fact below is from Base44's docs, Base44's pricing page or Base44's terms on 2026-11-24. Nothing was signed up for, installed, run, built or published. Prices, plans and terms change: check each page before you pay.

## Before your first prompt
- [ ] Training: "All other plans, including Business: your data can be used to train AI models." "There is no opt-out setting to find or switch on." On Enterprise, "your data is not used to train AI models", automatically, and "The Enterprise exclusion covers your whole workspace [all your apps], including personal information that people submit through your app's forms." https://docs.base44.com/Community-and-support/Privacy-and-security
- [ ] Your data choices: to remove an app, Dashboard, Settings, Danger Zone, Delete App (it stays in Recently deleted for 30 days, then it's gone). To delete your whole account, or to ask for a copy of the personal data Base44 holds about you, see the same Privacy and security page.
- [ ] The terms (4.2) license Customer Data to "train Company software tools (e.g. artificial intelligence and machine learning models)". Ownership (5.3): "the Customer owns all rights, title and interest in the code and applications generated by the Platform", "to the extent such rights exist under applicable law". https://base44.com/terms-of-service
- [ ] Keep real customer names and passwords out of every prompt: made-up test data only.

## Plans and credits (when checked, 2026-11-24)
- [ ] Free: $0, 25 message credits a month (up to 5 a day), 100 integration credits a month, up to 5 apps. Starter $16, Builder $40, Pro $80, Elite $160 a month, billed annually (yearly saves 20%). https://base44.com/pricing
- [ ] The first build uses "about 1 credit"; after that, each change costs credits by how much work it is. Clicking around the preview is free. https://docs.base44.com/Getting-Started/Quick-start-guide

## 1. One prompt
- [ ] Say what a customer does, ask for sign up and log in with email and password, say each customer sees only their own orders, say what to save, and use made-up data.

## 2. Sign-ups
- [ ] In the preview, sign up with an email inbox of your own. Base44 sends a 6-digit code: it works for 10 minutes, 5 tries. Not there? Spam folder, then Resend. https://docs.base44.com/Setting-up-your-app/Managing-login-and-registration
- [ ] Dashboard, Settings, Authentication: Email and password on. Other ways in: Google, Microsoft, Facebook, Apple (switch on, then Send to chat to add the button).
- [ ] The default Google sign-in window is "branded with base44.com"; your own Google sign-in needs "a Builder plan or higher" and Google's approval.
- [ ] Phone apps: Apple's rules "generally require" Sign in with Apple when you offer Google, Microsoft or Facebook. Turn Apple on and publish before you send the iPhone files.

## 3. Who can see what
- [ ] Dashboard, Data, your orders table, Permissions (top right). Base44's example: "orders stay visible only to the person who placed them and your admins". Look for Creator Only (a person reaches only the entries they created). https://docs.base44.com/Setting-up-your-app/Managing-security-settings
- [ ] A "Permission risks detected" banner on a table? Click More details to see the risk, or Fix to apply the suggestion.
- [ ] More actions (top bar), Act as a user, your test customer: they see their own orders and nobody else's.

## 4. Publish
- [ ] Public: "Anyone on the internet". Private: "Only invited people", Starter plan or higher. https://docs.base44.com/Setting-up-your-app/Managing-access
- [ ] On a public app, the Edit with Base44 badge lets "anyone who clicks it" copy your app into their own account. Starter or higher hides it, which "also stops people copying your app".
- [ ] Publish (top right). The panel opens on the Web tab, with Mobile app beside it. Copy your link, or change the part before .base44.app. Get domain: Starter plan or higher. https://docs.base44.com/Getting-Started/Quick-start-guide
- [ ] Open your link in a private browser window, sign up with a second email of your own, place an order.
- [ ] Changed something? Publish again: your published app "only changes when you publish".

## 5. Phone stores
- [ ] Your phone app is "a lightweight native wrapper around your web app": it runs your published app "inside a secure web view". https://docs.base44.com/documentation/building-your-app/uploading-to-app-stores
- [ ] You set up and pay for your own Apple Developer Program and Google Play developer accounts. Apple's activation can take up to 2 business days.
- [ ] Publish, Mobile app, Check Your App, Run App Scan (App Store or Google Play guidelines). Runs on the Free plan. Apply with AI, or Copy Fix Prompt; scan again.
- [ ] Store files need "a Builder plan or higher". Build Stores Files, Create App Store files (Issuer ID, Key ID, Team ID and the API key file from App Store Connect) or Create Google Play files, then Generate Files, Download.
- [ ] Push notifications (alerts on someone's phone, even when the app is closed): turn on Add push notifications in the same window, before Generate Files. iPhone needs an APNs auth key from your Apple Developer account (a different .p8 file from the App Store Connect one, also downloadable once); Android needs google-services.json and a service account key from your own Firebase project. Then send them with the SendPushNotification integration.
- [ ] Apple's key file downloads once. Never paste the IDs or the key file into the chat or share them; if one leaks, revoke it in App Store Connect.
- [ ] Send the files in App Store Connect and in your Google Play developer account. "Base44 cannot guarantee that an app is approved, even with a high readiness score."
- [ ] Selling in your app: physical goods and real-world services (a cake, a booking) can use any payment provider; digital items (subscriptions, unlocking features, credits) can't be sold in the phone app yet, the docs say.

## After the stores
- [ ] Most changes reach the phone app as soon as you publish in Base44. New store files only when the name, icon or splash screen changes, or when you add push notifications or a feature that needs new phone permissions, and make fresh files each time: the stores turn down a build number they've already seen.
```
