# Notion AI vs ChatGPT vs Claude for your notes: decision checklist (facts checked 2026-11-18)

To keep a copy: paste this into Notepad and save it with the box called Save as type set to All files. On a Mac, open TextEdit, choose Format, then Make Plain Text, before you paste and save.

Every line is from that company's own page, fetched on 2026-11-18. Plans, prices and dates change: check each line again on the page named before you pay. ## What you need
- [ ] To know where most of your notes are: in Notion, or somewhere else.
- [ ] If Notion: which plan you are on (Free, Plus, Business or Enterprise), and whether it is yours or your team's.
- [ ] A list of what you already pay for: Notion, ChatGPT, Claude.
- [ ] For work: your employer's rules on which tools you may put work notes into.

## 1. Where it works
- [ ] Notion AI: inside Notion, on your pages. Notion: "Notion AI is available on Business and Enterprise Plans."
- [ ] ChatGPT and Claude: chat apps. Notion's help page: "Connect your favorite AI apps, like Claude, ChatGPT, and Cursor, directly to your Notion workspace."
- [ ] A connected app can open what you can. Notion: "Remember that MCP tools act with your full Notion permissions. They can access everything you can access."

## 2. Which plan gets you an assistant over your Notion pages
- [ ] Notion AI: Business and Enterprise. Free and Plus list a "Trial of Notion AI": a limited number of responses, with no number published.
- [ ] The connection the chat apps go through: Notion's developer docs say "Notion MCP works on every plan." No charge for it is listed by Notion.
- [ ] ChatGPT: OpenAI's pricing table lists Plugins as Yes on Free, Go, Plus and Pro. It doesn't name Notion, so which group a Notion plugin is in was not stated on 2026-11-18: look in ChatGPT's own plugin list on your plan.
- [ ] Claude: Anthropic's pricing table lists Connectors as Yes on Free, Pro, Max and Ultra. Its help page says "Web connectors are available for all users on Claude, Cowork, Claude Desktop, and Claude Mobile (iOS and Android)." The Notion connector is made by Notion, and its page names no plan: look in Claude's own connector list on your plan.

## 3. Prices, in US dollars, when checked 2026-11-18

- [ ] Capturing what people say in a meeting may need their consent, depending on where you are. Tell everyone before you start.
