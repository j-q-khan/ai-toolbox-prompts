```
# Paste this into whichever one you're trying: ChatGPT with Work switched on, Claude, or Comet Assistant. The same words work in all three.
# ChatGPT desktop app: choose ChatGPT, then use the toggle above the message box to pick Work. Claude: type in the message box (select Cowork if you see the option). Comet: open the Assistant panel beside your page.
# It needs no websites, no plugins and no files. It doesn't pick a product for you or quote prices.
# Written from each company's help pages, checked 2026-11-19, not run. Check what it says against each company's own pricing and help pages before you decide.
# To keep a copy: paste this into Notepad and save it with the box called Save as type set to All files.
# On a Mac: open TextEdit, choose Format, then Make Plain Text, before you paste and save.
# Leave these lines starting with # out when you paste: copy from the word Help to the end.

Help me work out which kind of AI helper fits how I work.
Don't recommend a product and don't quote prices. Help me describe myself, then stop.

Rules for this whole task:
1. Ask me before you visit a website, sign in, buy, send or delete anything. This task needs no websites.
2. Never ask me for a password, a card number or any payment detail, and never ask me to type one into this chat.
3. Ask me the five questions below one at a time, and wait for each answer. If an answer is unclear or missing, ask me again before you go on.

The five questions:
1. Which web browser do I use most, and would I be happy to switch to a new browser?
2. Which AI plans do I already pay for, if any (for example a ChatGPT, Claude or Perplexity plan)?
3. Where do I mostly work: on a phone, on a computer (Windows, Mac or Linux), or both?
4. What is the first job I would hand over, and does it need files on my computer, websites, or neither?
5. How closely do I want to watch: approve every step, check in now and then, or come back to a finished file?

When I have answered all five:
1. Describe the type of person my answers fit in two or three sentences, for example "someone who already pays for one plan and wants finished files from one request" or "someone who wants help on the page in front of them".
2. List what I should check on each company's own pricing and help pages before I decide, with no prices filled in.
3. Remind me that plans and prices change, and that I should check those pages myself.
Then stop.
```

```
# ChatGPT Work vs Claude Cowork vs Perplexity Comet: choosing checklist (facts checked 2026-11-19, from each company's own pages)

To keep a copy: paste this into Notepad and save it with the box called Save as type set to All files. On a Mac, open TextEdit, choose Format, then Make Plain Text, before you paste and save.

## Before you pay: check each company's own page
- [ ] Which plan you already have. Work on ChatGPT Free and Go is "Limited (desktop app)"; Plus and Pro list "Desktop, web, and mobile". https://chatgpt.com/pricing
- [ ] Cowork is "…available to paid Claude plans (Pro, Max, Ultra, Team, Enterprise) only." https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13345190-get-started-with-claude-cowork and https://claude.com/pricing
- [ ] Comet: the blog (2 October 2025) says the browser was released "for free"; the plans page lists Browser Agent (Comet Assistant) queries as "No" on Free. Check the plans page on the day: https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/11187416-which-perplexity-subscription-plan-is-right-for-you
- [ ] Your device. ChatGPT desktop app: macOS, Windows or Linux. Claude Desktop: macOS, Windows, and Linux (beta). Comet: its page lists Mac, Windows, iOS and Android, while its system requirements page lists Windows 10 and 11 and macOS 13 or later, and says iOS and Android aren't officially supported.
- [ ] On a work or school account: check what your employer or school allows first. ChatGPT Work "…follows the privacy, security, and data-handling policies applicable to your ChatGPT workspace." Cowork on Team and Enterprise depends on what the owner has turned on.

## Try one safely
- [ ] Start with the worksheet prompt (which-one-fits-me.txt). It needs no websites and no files.
- [ ] Use made-up data for a first task. Never paste passwords, card numbers or codes into any chat.
- [ ] ChatGPT Work: leave Settings, then Cloud browser on Always ask or Auto approve; OpenAI does not recommend Always allow. https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/browser
- [ ] Claude Cowork: Anthropic says to switch to "Manually approve" when "The task touches sensitive files, accounts, or sites." Connect a test folder, not your whole drive. https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13364135-use-claude-cowork-safely
- [ ] Perplexity Comet: when an agent first runs you'll be asked "Allow this time only", "Always allow" or "Don't allow". You can block sites in Settings, then Privacy and security. https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/comet/en/articles/12658082-control-what-comet-assistant-can-use

## Check it worked
- [ ] The worksheet asked you all five questions one at a time, and named a type of person without picking a product or quoting a price.
- [ ] You checked the type it named against each company's own pricing page, on the day.

## Afterwards: what to switch off or clear
- [ ] ChatGPT Work: Settings, then Cloud browser, then Browser data: delete one site, or Clear all (signs you out of every site in Work's browser).
- [ ] Claude Cowork: delete a task with the "Delete" option (click the three dots next to the task); Anthropic says it is removed from your task history immediately and deleted from its storage within 30 days.
- [ ] Perplexity Comet: Settings, then Privacy and security, then Comet Assistant: block the Assistant on specific websites, or turn it off; Settings, then Privacy and security, then Delete browsing data.
```
