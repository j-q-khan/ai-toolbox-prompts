# Gemini for Home and Work, Part 4: Gemini for Studying: How to Use Guided Learning, Step by Step

Free files for the video. Facts checked 2026-11-27. Google changes its buttons and menus often, so open each source before you rely on a line.

- `guided-learning-study-prompts.txt`: a study-plan prompt for any topic, plus prompts for a diagram, practice questions with hints, a quiz, flashcards and getting unstuck, with where to paste them. Fill in the square brackets with your own topic, level and goal.
- `gemini-study-checklist.md`: a quiz-yourself checklist for a Guided Learning session: before you start, during the session, testing yourself, and who can use what, in Google's words.
- `BUILD-PROMPT.md`: paste it into Claude Code in an empty folder. It builds a one-page study-prompt maker with a quiz-yourself flashcard page. It uses only the facts in the prompt, downloads nothing and runs no third-party code.
- `CODE.md`: the files above, exactly as shown in the video.
- `SOURCES.md`: every page the facts come from, with the date it was checked.

Everything in the video was written from Google's help pages and blog. Nothing was tried on a real account, and nothing was signed up for, signed in to or run. Outputs shown are labelled "Example output", and the topics are examples. Not affiliated with Google.
