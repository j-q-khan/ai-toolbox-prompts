# Should you switch to Gemini for Home? Checklist (facts checked 2026-11-26)

To keep a copy: paste this into Notepad and save it with the box called Save as type set to All files, as gemini-for-home-checklist.md. On a Mac, open TextEdit, choose Format, then Make Plain Text, before you paste and save.

## Before anything: it's one-way
- [ ] Google: "Once you switch your home to Gemini for Home voice assistant, it can't switch back to Google Assistant."

## 1. Your speakers and displays
Full experience, including Gemini Live:
- [ ] Google Home Speaker (2026)
- [ ] Google Nest Hub (2nd gen)
- [ ] Google Nest Audio
- [ ] Google Nest Mini (2nd gen)
- [ ] Google Nest Hub Max
- [ ] Google Nest Hub (1st gen)
- [ ] Google Home Mini (1st gen)
Most features, but not Gemini Live:
- [ ] Google Nest Wifi point
- [ ] Google Home Max
- [ ] Google Home
- [ ] Third-party speakers
Firmware: speakers Cast firmware 3.76 or higher; displays Fuchsia firmware 28 or higher.

## 2. Your country (early access)
- [ ] Now: Australia, Austria, Belgium, Canada, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Ireland, Italy, Japan, Mexico, Netherlands, New Zealand, Norway, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, United Kingdom, United States.
- [ ] Coming in December 2026: Brazil, Colombia, India, Singapore, South Korea, Taiwan.
- [ ] Languages now: Danish, Dutch, English, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Norwegian, Spanish, Swedish.

## 3. Your accounts
- [ ] Everyone uses a personal Google account.
- [ ] Not yet supported: Google Workspace (work) accounts and Family Link child accounts.

## 4. Your app
- [ ] Google Home app version 4.1 or newer (Android or iPhone).
- [ ] Android: update Speech Recognition and Synthesis first.

## 5. What's free, what costs
- [ ] Free: smart home controls, media, alarms and timers, calendars, notes, lists, reminders, and questions on a wide range of topics.
- [ ] Google Home Premium: Gemini Live, Help me create (automations).
- [ ] Advanced plan only: camera AI descriptions, AI notifications, Home Brief.
- [ ] Prices in Google's launch post (Oct 1, 2025, US dollars): Standard $10 a month or $100 a year; Advanced $20 a month or $200 a year. Newest US price we could open on 2026-11-26. Your country: https://store.google.com/product/google_home_premium
- [ ] Google AI Pro includes the Standard plan.

## 6. Switch (only when you're sure)
- [ ] Tap the "Introducing Gemini for Home" notification, then Get started; or profile picture (top right), Home settings, the banner, Get started.
- [ ] During setup: response filtering. Default: filtered for guests; voice-matched kids always filtered.

## 7. Privacy
- [ ] Activity saves to Home History in My Activity (with Voice Match and Home History on).
- [ ] Default auto-delete: 18 months. Change to 3 or 36 months, or off: profile picture, Home settings, Privacy, Home History.
- [ ] On a computer: https://myactivity.google.com/product/home
- [ ] Voice and audio setting: off by default; needs Home History on.

## 8. Wait if
- [ ] Anyone at home uses a Workspace or Family Link child account.
- [ ] You have the original Google Home or Home Max and want Gemini Live.
- [ ] Your country isn't on the list yet.
