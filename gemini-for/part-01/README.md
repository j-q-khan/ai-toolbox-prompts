# Gemini for Home and Work, Part 1: Gemini for Home Review: Should You Switch From Google Assistant? (You Can't Switch Back)

Free files for the video. Facts checked 2026-11-26. Which speakers get it, the plans and the prices change often, so open each source before you rely on a line.

- `gemini-for-home-checklist.md`: a dated checklist for deciding whether to switch: the one-way warning in Google's words, which speakers and displays get the full experience, what's free and what needs Google Home Premium, Home History and its auto-delete, and who should wait.
- `gemini-for-home-settings.txt`: where each setting is in the Google Home app, written from Google Home Help.
- `BUILD-PROMPT.md`: paste it into Claude Code in an empty folder. It builds a one-page "should I switch now or wait?" helper from the dated facts. It uses only the facts in the prompt, downloads nothing and runs no third-party code.
- `CODE.md`: the files above, exactly as shown in the video.
- `SOURCES.md`: every page the facts come from, with the date it was checked.

Everything in the video was written from Google's help pages. Nothing was tried on a real home, and nothing was signed up for, signed in to or run. Outputs shown are labelled "Example output". Not affiliated with Google.
