# Gemini for Home: where each setting is, in the Google Home app.
# Written from Google Home Help (answers 16618650 and 16613583), checked 2026-11-26. Not tried on a real home.
# To keep a copy: paste into Notepad (Save as type: All files) or TextEdit (Format, Make Plain Text first).

# Switch to Gemini for Home (one-way: it can't switch back to Google Assistant)
Notification: Introducing Gemini for Home → Get started
Profile picture or initial (top right) → Home settings → banner "Set up Gemini for Home for more natural conversations" → Get started

# Gemini for Home settings, and the voice
Home settings → Gemini for Home voice assistant
Home settings → Gemini for Home voice assistant → Voice selection

# Home History (kept 18 months by default; 3 or 36 months, or off)
Profile picture → Home settings → Privacy → Home History
On a computer: myactivity.google.com/product/home

# The voice and audio setting (off by default; needs Home History on). Its exact name:
Improve Google services with your voice, audio, and Gemini Live recordings
