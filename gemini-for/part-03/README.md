# Gemini for Home and Work, Part 3: Gemini for Work in Google Sheets and Meet: the AI Formula and Take Notes for Me

Free files for the video. Facts checked 2026-11-27. Which plan gets which feature, and the prices, change often, so open each source before you rely on a line.

- `gemini-sheets-ai-formulas.txt`: ready AI function formulas for Google Sheets, with the cell to paste each one into and the Generate and insert step.
- `practice-comments.csv`: ten made-up customer comments for a made-up bakery, to try the formulas on. In Google Sheets: File, then Import, choose the file, then Import.
- `gemini-sheets-build-prompts.txt`: prompts for Build, the Gemini side panel in Google Sheets.
- `gemini-meet-notes-checklist.md`: a dated checklist for Take notes for me in Google Meet: whose plan counts, where to click, who's told, where the notes go, and how to switch it on ahead of time.
- `BUILD-PROMPT.md`: paste it into Claude Code in an empty folder. It builds the practice sheet and a one-page helper with the formulas to copy and the meeting checklist. It uses only the facts in the prompt, downloads nothing and runs no third-party code.
- `CODE.md`: the files above, exactly as shown in the video.
- `SOURCES.md`: every page the facts come from, with the date it was checked.

Everything in the video was written from Google's help pages. Nothing was tried on a real account, and nothing was signed up for, signed in to or run. Outputs shown are labelled "Example output", and every name, shop, comment and email address is made up. Not affiliated with Google.
