# Full code: Google AI Mode: How to Use It in Search (and Delete Its History)

The prompt list and the sample questions for this video, written from Google's AI Mode help page (checked 2026-11-24). They were written from the docs, not run, and nothing was generated for this video.

### 1. ai-mode-prompts.txt
```
# Prompts that work well in Google AI Mode, written for this video (checked 2026-11-24). Not run: nothing was generated.
# To keep a copy: paste this into Notepad and save it as ai-mode-prompts.txt with the box called Save as type set to All files.
# On a Mac: open TextEdit, choose Format, then Make Plain Text, before you paste and save.
# Go to google.com/ai, paste one prompt into the Ask anything bar, then type the Then ask line as your follow-up.
# Why full sentences: Google's help page says AI Mode divides your question into subtopics (query fan-out), so details help.
# Keep personal details (passwords, account numbers, your home address) out of every prompt.
# Google's help page: "AI Mode doesn't always get it right." Check important info in more than one place.

A rain jacket for cycling to work in heavy rain, under $100. Give me the main options and links to the pages you used.
Then ask: Which of those has a hood that fits over a helmet?

Plan a three-day trip to Lisbon in March for two people who like food markets and walking, on a mid-range budget.
Then ask: Which day works best if it rains?

My basil plant on a windowsill has yellow leaves at the bottom and gets about four hours of sun. What could be causing it?
Then ask: How often should I water it in winter?

Compare three budget laptops for a student who writes essays and watches videos, with battery life and weight for each.
Then ask: Which one would you avoid, and why?

Explain how a heat pump works, like I'm new to it, and what it costs to run compared with a gas boiler.
Then ask: What should I check before I get one fitted?

# Delete what you asked (computer): AI Mode history on the left, then More, then Delete; or More at the top, then Delete all.
# Deleted searches may still appear in My Activity for some time; Google says they're auto-deleted in less than 24 hours.
# Began as an AI Overview (Dive deeper in AI Mode)? Delete it from your Search history too.
```
