THE COST

[ ] Our note: outside the U.S. and Canada, check the price the page shows you.

MY ANSWER (tick one)

[ ] I'm mainly curious and don't need a certificate. Our note: start somewhere cheaper, and compare beginner courses first (AI for Beginners, Part 4: https://youtu.be/MRw58nvN3g4).
