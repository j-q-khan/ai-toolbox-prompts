# Which AI assistant app: the checklist (facts checked 2026-11-23)

To keep a copy: paste this into Notepad and save it as which-ai-assistant-checklist.md with the box called Save as type set to All files. On a Mac: open TextEdit, choose Format, then Make Plain Text, before you paste and save.

Every fact below is from the company's own page on 2026-11-23. Nothing was signed up for, installed or run. Menus move and prices change: check each page before you rely on it. Prices are US dollars before tax.

## 1. Start with your device
- [ ] iPhone: Siri. Siri AI is in beta in English with iOS 27 (Apple, September 14, 2026), its models "custom-built in collaboration with Google and its Gemini models". Needs an Apple Intelligence device: check https://support.apple.com/en-us/121115
- [ ] Android: Gemini (Google). Android, iPhone, the web (gemini.google.com) and a desktop app for Windows and Mac. https://gemini.google/desktop/
- [ ] Windows PC: the Copilot key opens Microsoft Copilot (or Windows search when Copilot isn't available). https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/keyboard-shortcuts-in-windows-dcc61a57-8ff0-cffe-9796-cb9706c75eec
- [ ] Mac: Siri, plus Mac apps from ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Copilot.

## 2. Match the app to your everyday job
- [ ] iPhone and want a full chat app behind Siri: ChatGPT (OpenAI), Siri's one extension. https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/use-chatgpt-with-apple-intelligence-iph00fd3c8c2/ios
- [ ] Gmail and Android: Gemini (Google).
- [ ] Word, Excel and Outlook: Copilot (Microsoft). "Copilot is available in Microsoft 365 Personal, Family, Premium, and Pro subscriptions". https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-copilot/copilot-in-microsoft-365-personal-family-and-premium
- [ ] Documents and your own files, any device: Claude (Anthropic). Free plan: "Search the web, create files, and run code", "Connect your apps and tools". https://claude.com/pricing
- [ ] Also around: Meta AI ("Meta AI will still be free for everyday use", Meta, September 15, 2026) and Alexa+ ("free with Prime"; without Prime it was $19.99 a month when checked on 2026-11-23).

## 3. Plans: before paying, then the cheapest paid step
| App | Before paying (maker's words) | Cheapest paid plan, was ... when checked on 2026-11-23 | Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| Claude | Free: "Free for everyone", "Limited usage every 5 hours and every week" | Pro: $20 a month billed monthly | https://claude.com/pricing |
| Gemini | Free: "$0/ month with a Google Account" | Google AI Plus: $4.99 a month | https://gemini.google/us/subscriptions/?hl=en |
| ChatGPT | Free: "Limited voice chats", "Limited deep research", "Limited memory and context" | Go: $8 a month in the US (OpenAI's January 16, 2026 post; OpenAI planned to test ads in Free and Go in the US) | https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-go/ |
| Copilot | No no-cost plan spelled out; Microsoft 365 Personal gives "higher usage than free" | Microsoft 365 Personal: $9.99 a month (the page showed $6.99 for the first month as a "Limited-time offer", renewing "at the regular price") | https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-copilot/for-individuals |

## 4. Switch off training before your first real chat
- [ ] Gemini: Settings, History, untick "Chat content including text & uploads" (on by default if you are over 18; History must be on to change it). Reviewed chats are kept up to 3 years, even if you delete them. https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13278892
- [ ] ChatGPT: account menu, Settings, Data controls, turn off "Improve the model for everyone", Done (OpenAI: "data sharing is enabled by default" on personal Free, Plus and Pro). Phone app: profile icon, Data controls. https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7730893-data-controls-faq
- [ ] Claude: "Model Improvement" under Privacy Settings: https://claude.ai/settings/data-privacy-controls
- [ ] Copilot: "Training on conversation activity" and "Training on voice conversations" (computer app: profile icon, Settings, Privacy). Microsoft says that page covers only the older app (a newer app arrived August 18, 2026); if your menus differ, see https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/privacy/microsoft-copilot/overview
- [ ] Work or school account: follow your employer's rules first.
- [ ] A one-off question: ChatGPT's Temporary chat (new chat, Temporary) is "not used to improve OpenAI models while it remains temporary"; OpenAI may keep a copy up to 30 days for safety.

## 5. Make it your default
- [ ] Android: Settings, Apps, Default apps, Digital assistant app (or Default digital assistant app), the Google app. Then Settings, Apps, Assistant, Digital assistants from Google, Gemini. https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14554984
- [ ] iPhone: Settings, Siri, ChatGPT, Set Up. With the Siri AI beta: Set Up Extensions, ChatGPT, Use Extension. Signing in is optional, needed only to save requests to your ChatGPT history.
- [ ] Windows: Settings, Personalization, Text input, Customize Copilot key on keyboard.
