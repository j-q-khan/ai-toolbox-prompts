# How to use Gemini Notebook (NotebookLM): checklist (facts checked 2026-11-18)

To keep a copy: paste this into Notepad and save it with the box called Save as type set to All files. On a Mac, open TextEdit, choose Format, then Make Plain Text, before you paste and save.

## What you need
- [ ] A Google Account and a web browser. It's a website, so the steps are the same on Windows and Mac.
- [ ] Sources you have the right to use. Google's sources page: "Avoid uploading documents you don't have rights to."
- [ ] For work or school: follow your employer's or school's rules first. No private or client documents.
- [ ] Your plan: Google's plans page lists "Gemini Notebook without an AI plan" at 100 notebooks and 50 sources per notebook (checked 2026-11-18, marked "Subject to Change"). Check support.google.com/gemininotebook/answer/16213268.

## Make a notebook and add sources
- [ ] Open notebook.google.com.
- [ ] Select Create new notebook.
- [ ] In the pop-up window, select Upload a source, and pick a file.
- [ ] Check: the file appears in the Sources panel, on the left.
- [ ] To add more: in the Sources panel, select Add sources (Google's Create a notebook page calls it + Add), then give it a web page link or a YouTube link. Several links: put a space or a new line between them.
- [ ] One source: up to 500,000 words, or 200MB for an uploaded file.
- [ ] Web pages: only the text is taken in. "Paywalled webpages aren't supported."
- [ ] YouTube: only public videos with captions. Only the transcript is imported. A video uploaded in the last 72 hours may not be available yet.
- [ ] Other source types Google lists: audio files, pasted text, Google Docs, Google Slides (up to 100 slides), Google Sheets, images, Word, text, Markdown, CSV, PowerPoint and ePub files, notes, eligible Play Books, and Gemini chats.
- [ ] Each notebook is independent: it can't look across two notebooks at once. One topic per notebook.

## Ask, and check the citation
- [ ] In the Chat panel, type a question about your sources and send it.
- [ ] Check: the answer carries citations.
- [ ] Hover over a citation for the full quoted text. Select it to jump to that quote in the source.
- [ ] If the quote doesn't back the answer up, don't rely on the answer. Google's chat page: "Gemini Notebook can make mistakes and do unexpected things. Be sure to double check it."
- [ ] To keep an answer: select Save to note. The note appears in the Sources panel.
- [ ] To write your own note: in the Sources panel, select Create note.
- [ ] To answer from only some sources: untick the others in the Sources panel, and name the source in your question.

## Audio and Video Overviews
- [ ] Both are in the Studio panel: select Audio Overview or Video Overview.
- [ ] Audio Overview: Google says it may take a couple of minutes. Formats: Deep Dive (the default), The Brief, The Critique, The Debate.
- [ ] Video Overview: Google says it may take a while, sometimes more than 30 minutes. Cinematic is for ages 18 and over; visual styles are for ages 18 and over too.
- [ ] Google says both are AI-generated and may contain inaccuracies. Go back to the citations for anything that matters.

## Limits
- [ ] Google's usage-limits page says the quota refreshes every 5 hours until you reach your weekly limit. It gives no number of chats or overviews a day (checked 2026-11-18).
- [ ] To see yours: at the top right, select Settings, then Usage.

## Privacy, in Google's words
- [ ] "The content in Gemini Notebook will not be used to directly train our foundational AI models, unless you choose to provide feedback."
- [ ] A thumbs-up or thumbs-down: "we'll collect the associated content", it is "Reviewed by specially trained teams", and it is "retained for up to 3 years, disconnected from your Google Account".
- [ ] Work and school accounts (Google Workspace, Workspace for Education): Google says uploads, queries and responses "will not be reviewed by human reviewers even when you provide thumbs up or down feedback".

## Tidy up afterwards
- [ ] Chat history: in the Chat panel, open the three dots menu and select Delete Chat History.
- [ ] Sources you no longer want answers from: untick them in the Sources panel.
- [ ] Deleting a whole notebook: the help pages fetched for this checklist give no steps for it (checked 2026-11-18); look for the notebook's own menu on the start page.
