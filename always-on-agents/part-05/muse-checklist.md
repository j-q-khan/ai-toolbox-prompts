# Muse by Meta: setup and safety checklist (facts checked 2026-11-27)

To keep a copy: paste this into Notepad and save it with the box called Save as type set to All files. On a Mac, open TextEdit, choose Format, then Make Plain Text, before you paste and save.
Meta changes these pages often. Before you rely on a line, open its source and check the date. Settings names are written exactly as Meta's pages write them.

## 1. Can I get it?
- [ ] US and Canada: "Available today to people in the US and Canada who are 18 and over." Source: muse.ai/business
- [ ] UK: "Muse is available to anyone aged 18 and over in the UK on iOS, Android, muse.ai and WhatsApp." (November 23, 2026) Source: about.fb.com/news/2026/11/introducing-muse-in-the-uk-a-personal-ai-agent-you-control/
- [ ] Also listed: Japan (from October 23, 2026), Singapore (October 27, 2026), Australia (November 17, 2026). Sources: about.fb.com Japan, Singapore and Australia posts
- [ ] Somewhere else? Check Meta's newsroom for your country before you sign up.
- [ ] "It's free for most of what people need, with subscription plans for people who want to do more." Plan prices not checked: Meta's Help Center page showed only a cookie notice. Source: about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/

## 2. Sign up
- [ ] Get the Muse app on your phone, or go to muse.ai: "Log in or create an account" with "Mobile number or email". Source: muse.ai
- [ ] "You can sign up to Muse using a new account, or sign up with your existing Meta account. Facebook, Instagram and Threads are connected automatically if you have your accounts in the same Accounts Center." Source: about.fb.com/news/2026/11/muse-now-available-in-the-uk-muse-does-the-work-you-stay-in-control/
- [ ] While testing, use a new account and a throwaway email, never your real accounts. (Our advice.) To make one: support.google.com/accounts/answer/27441
- [ ] WhatsApp: "Chat with your agent using the Muse app on mobile, desktop or directly in WhatsApp, by message or with voice mode." No Meta page checked shows the steps for starting Muse in WhatsApp, and the Australia, Singapore and Japan posts don't mention it. Source: muse.ai

## 3. Choose what it can reach
- [ ] "You decide which Connectors your Muse can use, and how much access each one gets. You can add, or disconnect, a service whenever you want in your settings."
- [ ] Email: "you can let it read your email without allowing it to send anything."
- [ ] "Every Muse gets its own virtual computer in the cloud, like a laptop that's just for you. Your information and logins are kept there"
- [ ] Muse "can't see your passwords or card details".
- [ ] "If you connect your email, Muse also ignores one-time codes and password-reset links."

## 4. What it's designed to ask first
- [ ] "Muse checks with the person before sensitive actions like sending an email or making a purchase." Source: about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/
- [ ] "Purchases need your approval every time. For other tasks, you can choose whether to allow Muse to take steps without asking each time and how long your permission lasts." While testing, keep it asking. (Our advice.)
- [ ] "You can also choose how often it checks in with you in Settings."
- [ ] "Muse keeps a full record of everything it has done and plans to do next, along with every permission you've approved. When it's using the web for you, you can watch it work and take over at any point."
- [ ] "Muse can and will still make mistakes". Look over what it shows you before you say yes. Source: muse.ai/blog/security-and-safety-for-ai-agents-our-approach-with-muse

## 5. Ads and training
- [ ] "Your conversations with Muse, and the information it keeps for you, aren't shared with Meta's advertising systems."
- [ ] "When Muse browses the internet, it will appear as your activity, so if you ask Muse to buy a shirt from a clothing designer's website, that designer might use your visit to show you an ad on Instagram." Source: muse.ai/blog/security-and-safety-for-ai-agents-our-approach-with-muse
- [ ] AI training: "This setting is on when you start, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings under Data controls." (Meta's UK post)

## 6. Stop it and delete what it keeps
- [ ] Unprompted messages: "you can always tell your Muse to turn them off, dial them down or dial them up."
- [ ] "You can see and edit what it remembers at any time, tell it to forget a topic or a person, or reset Muse to delete all your Muse data and start again."
- [ ] Disconnect any service you no longer want it to reach, in your settings.
