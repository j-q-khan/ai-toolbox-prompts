# Task in Gemini (formerly Gemini Spark): setup and safety checklist (facts checked 2026-11-26)

To keep a copy: paste this into Notepad and save it with the box called Save as type set to All files. On a Mac, open TextEdit, choose Format, then Make Plain Text, before you paste and save.
Google changes these pages often. Before you rely on a line, open its source and check the date. Menu names are written exactly as Google's help pages write them.

## 1. Can I use it?
- [ ] Not everyone has it yet: "For now, assigning tasks to Gemini isn't available to everyone." Source: support.google.com/gemini/answer/17094196
- [ ] The rename, in Google's words: "That's the word that kept coming up, so we renamed it Task to make it easier to understand." Source: support.google.com/gemini/answer/17094507
- [ ] "Available without a Google AI subscription in Australia, Canada, India, Japan, South Korea, and the US."
- [ ] "Available with a Google AI Pro or Ultra subscription everywhere else Gemini Apps are supported, except in the European Economic Area, Nigeria, Switzerland, and the United Kingdom."
- [ ] "Be 18 or over." A personal Google account: "For now, this feature isn't available if you sign in with a work or school Google Account." "Have History on."
- [ ] Usage: "Gemini Apps have compute-based usage limits", and they rise with paid plans. Up to 15 tasks running at a time.
- [ ] Google's own words: "Gemini's ability to handle tasks is experimental." "Gemini is still learning and can make mistakes."
- [ ] Gemini Agent is a different tool: Google's release notes (2025.11.18): "Gemini Agent is now available on the web, to Google AI Ultra subscribers 18+ in the US."

## 2. Turn it on
- [ ] On a computer: go to gemini.google.com, then "Below the text box, click Task."
- [ ] On a phone or tablet: open the Gemini mobile app, then "Above the text box, tap Task."
- [ ] Not sure what to ask? "Just enter "set up," "get started," or "interview me" in a task."
- [ ] First job: one that needs no email or calendar. Google's example: "Get a custom news digest: Go deep on the stories you care about and follow how they evolve"
- [ ] Describe the job in the text box, then click Submit. To change course while it runs, type more instructions in the same box.

## 3. Choose what it can reach and what it asks
- [ ] Use a throwaway Google account made for testing, never your real inbox, until you've seen how it behaves. (Our advice.) To make one: support.google.com/accounts/answer/27441
- [ ] Connected apps: "These connections are turned off by default". Source: gemini.google/us/overview/agent/spark
- [ ] To switch one on: in the sidebar, click Customize, then Connectors ("If you don't find "Customize," first click More."), then click the app.
- [ ] Under "App actions", next to each action, pick "Always allow" or "Always ask". While testing, choose Always ask. (Our advice.) Source: support.google.com/gemini/answer/18766567
- [ ] Calendar: "Some Google Calendar actions, including updating events and sending invites, are designed to work automatically and Gemini will only check-in as needed."
- [ ] Google Tasks (the to-do app): "Gemini can perform bulk actions on private tasks in Google Tasks without your confirmation."
- [ ] The four answers in a task thread: Don't allow (stops that action); Allow once (this step only); Allow for this chat (the rest of this thread); Always allow (this thread and all future task threads, and it changes your setting to Always allow).
- [ ] What it's designed to ask about: "Gemini is designed to always ask for your review and confirmation before it completes certain actions, such as:" "Sending communications, deleting your data, sharing sensitive info, making purchases, completing financial transactions, and filling out and submitting web forms."
- [ ] Never type sign-in info or payment details into the thread: "If needed, take control of Gemini's browser or the local browser. Then, enter relevant details directly on the webpage."

## 4. Watch it work
- [ ] In the sidebar, each task's thread shows "Ready", "Needs attention" or "In progress". Source: support.google.com/gemini/answer/17094196
- [ ] Click the progress chip at the top of the thread: each step shows as "Completed step", "Current step" or "Planned step".
- [ ] Website jobs on a computer: "you'll need to keep your device and Google Chrome awake and running."
- [ ] First browsing job: "The first time Gemini needs to browse the web for you on a device, Chrome asks you for permission to connect Chrome auto browse to Gemini." "After that, for every task that involves web browsing, Gemini will ask for confirmation first."
- [ ] Choosing when it runs: "You can tell Gemini when to run the task, like around a specific time or after an event."
- [ ] Schedules without a paid plan: "Without a Google AI subscription, or with Google AI Plus, you can schedule tasks to run up to once a day during a general time window: overnight, daytime, or evening." Up to 10 active schedules. Source: support.google.com/gemini/answer/17094710

## 5. Stop it and delete it
- [ ] Cancel a task: "in the Gemini text box, click Stop."
- [ ] "If you deny an action in a task thread, Gemini stops and doesn't try another way to complete it."
- [ ] Delete a task: in the sidebar, hover over its thread, click More, then Delete. That deletes the task, its activity and its schedules.
- [ ] Deleting a thread does not delete "Any files that were created or modified like files in Google Workspace" or "Any remote browser data or remote code execution data associated with the task."
- [ ] Delete the saved sign-ins: Settings (at the bottom), then Browser, then next to "Delete remote browser data", click Delete.
- [ ] Turn task mode off: Settings (at the bottom), then Task settings, then under "Use task mode to hand off work", click Turn off. "Your remote browser data and remote code execution data is deleted." "Your schedules and tasks are paused and not deleted."
