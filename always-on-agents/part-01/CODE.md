# Which always-on AI agent fits you? A dated checklist (facts checked 2026-11-26)

To keep a copy: paste this into Notepad and save it with the box called Save as type set to All files. On a Mac, open TextEdit, choose Format, then Make Plain Text, before you paste and save.
These products changed every few weeks in 2026. Before you rely on a line, open its source and check the date. Prices are what each page showed when checked, in US dollars, before tax.

## Before you try any of them
- [ ] Use a throwaway account made for testing: never your real email, WhatsApp, work chat or bank account, until you've seen how the agent behaves.
- [ ] Find the agent's own stop before you start. Grok Bot's FAQ: "Closing the app, laptop, or phone does not stop a background turn or routine." (docs.x.ai/grok-bot/faq.md, 2026-11-26)
- [ ] Find its approval setting (the last part of each section below) before you connect any app.

## Task in Gemini (Google; called Gemini Spark until Google renamed it)
- [ ] Rename, in Google's words: "we renamed it Task to make it easier to understand." Source: support.google.com/gemini/answer/17094507
- [ ] Who can get it: "Available without a Google AI subscription in Australia, Canada, India, Japan, South Korea, and the US." Elsewhere: "Available with a Google AI Pro or Ultra subscription everywhere else Gemini Apps are supported", "except in the European Economic Area, Nigeria, Switzerland, and the United Kingdom." Age: "Be 18 or over." Not with a work or school Google Account "for now".
- [ ] Price: "rolling out free of charge" in the U.S., Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia and India (Google blog, Nov 17, 2026). Pro or Ultra prices for other countries: not quoted here.
- [ ] Where you use it: the Gemini mobile app, the Gemini app on Mac, and gemini.google.com. Start it by flipping the home-screen toggle from Chat to Task.
- [ ] What it can reach: Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, YouTube and Google Maps, plus 50+ third-party apps such as OpenTable and Instacart. "These connections are turned off by default", Google says, and you turn them on in Gemini's settings. Source: gemini.google/us/overview/agent/spark
- [ ] How it asks: Google says Gemini "is designed to always ask for your review and confirmation before it completes certain actions, such as" "Sending communications, deleting your data, sharing sensitive info, making purchases, completing financial transactions, and filling out and submitting web forms." Up to 15 tasks at a time.

## Grok Bot (xAI, the company behind the Grok chatbot)
- [ ] Who can get it: included with The X Subscription's Premium, Super and Ultra plans. "Plus does not include Grok Bot." No country list or age rule on the Grok Bot pages checked. x.ai/bot says "Grok Bot is out of beta." X Premium bought on X is a different plan: xAI's page "X Premium Customers moving to The X Subscription" says it's cancelled when you switch (docs.x.ai/x-subscription/x-premium).
- [ ] Price, when checked 2026-11-26: Plus $8/mo (no Grok Bot); Premium $30/mo ($300/yr); Super $100/mo ($1000/yr); Ultra $200/mo ($2000/yr). "List prices do not include sales tax." Source: docs.x.ai/x-subscription/pricing
- [ ] Where you use it: "the Grok Bot desktop app on macOS, Windows, or Linux, or the companion app on iOS or Android"; Team Bots can join Slack and Microsoft Teams. Source: docs.x.ai/grok-bot/faq.md
- [ ] What it can reach: "Bots can use a persistent cloud computer, connected tools, websites, and files". Source: docs.x.ai/grok-bot/faq.md
- [ ] How it asks: the Auto-review switch is in Settings, General, under Bot, and "Auto Review is on by default." Custom rules: "Ask first" and "Allow automatically"; "If both kinds of rule match, Ask first wins." Source: docs.x.ai/grok-bot/approvals-security-and-privacy

## Claude Duet (Anthropic, the company that makes Claude)
- [ ] Status: research preview, by waitlist. "A research preview is available by waitlist today, starting with US Pro, Max, and Ultra subscribers." (Oct 21, 2026)
- [ ] Countries, two pages disagree: the help article says "Pro, Max, and Ultra plans in the US, through a waitlist" and "Claude Duet is available in the US only during the research preview."; the product page says "in the US, India, Indonesia, Brazil & Japan." Not on Team or Enterprise plans yet. No page lists Europe.
- [ ] Price: "Claude Duet is included in Pro, Max, and Ultra plans". Pro was $20 billed monthly ($17 a month with $200 billed up front); Max $100 a month; Ultra $200 a month, when checked 2026-11-26. Its background checks count toward your plan's usage limits. Source: claude.com/pricing
- [ ] Where you use it: its own app on iPhone and Android, and on the web at claude.ai.
- [ ] What it can reach: "Works with more than 6,000 connectors and plugins". "It doesn't make phone calls or send text messages to other people."
- [ ] How it asks: "before it sends a message, makes a payment, signs a document, or deletes anything, it shows you exactly what it's about to do and waits for your approval." Off-limits even with approval: "trading securities" and "banking actions such as moving money between accounts". No pause switch or approval setting name is documented.

## Muse (Meta, the company that owns Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp)
- [ ] Who can get it: US and Canada, 18 and over (muse.ai/business); Singapore (Oct 27, 2026); Australia (Nov 17, 2026); UK, "anyone aged 18 and over" (Nov 23, 2026). No Meta page we found mentions the EU.
- [ ] Price: "It's free to use, with optional subscription plans for those who want more usage." (UK post, Nov 23, 2026). Plan prices: Meta's help page didn't open for us, so not quoted.
- [ ] Where you use it: "the Muse app on mobile, desktop or directly in WhatsApp, by message or with voice mode." Source: muse.ai
- [ ] What it can reach: "Connect your email and calendar and it can help you sort your inbox". In Australia Meta names Gmail, Google Calendar, Instagram and OpenTable. It runs on the Muse Secure VM, its own cloud computer.
- [ ] How it asks: "Muse asks for your go-ahead before important actions, like sending an email or making a purchase." Conversations and VM data "aren't shared with Meta's advertising systems."

## ChatGPT dots (OpenAI, the company that makes ChatGPT)
- [ ] What a dot is, in OpenAI's words: for "When you want an agent to keep track of an ongoing responsibility"; ChatGPT Work is for "When you have a task to delegate". Source: learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots.md
- [ ] Who can get it: "Pro and selected Plus users aged 18 and older in markets excluding the European Economic Area, Switzerland, and the UK." Plus is a gradual rollout. Business Premium and Business Standard seats in supported regions also qualify. Source: help.openai.com/en/articles/20001530
- [ ] Price: "Your first dot is included in your Pro or Business Premium plan at no extra cost." Pro 100 was $100, Pro 200 $200, Pro 500 $500 a month when checked 2026-11-26; "Pro is billed monthly." Source: help.openai.com/en/articles/9793128
- [ ] What it can reach: "their own cloud computer, their own browser, and the apps you've connected"; "over 4,000 apps"; message it in Slack and Teams. Source: openai.com/index/introducing-dots
- [ ] How it asks: Custom rules (on mobile, Customize, then Custom rules) with four choices: "Take action without asking", "Take action if pre-approved" (the learn page says "Take action when you say so"), "Ask before taking action", "Hand off to you". Pause is in the more options menu (•••) on its profile; "Pausing your dot does not delete its information."

## Which one fits me?
- [ ] In the US, Canada, Australia, India, Japan or South Korea, with a personal Google account, and want a free one: Task.
- [ ] In the UK and want a free one: Muse (the one of the five with a UK page saying it's offered).
- [ ] On The X Subscription's Premium plan or higher (a different plan from X Premium bought on X): Grok Bot is included.
- [ ] On Claude Pro, Max or Ultra in the US: join the Claude Duet waitlist (a research preview).
- [ ] Already on ChatGPT Pro, outside the EEA, Switzerland and the UK: your first dot is included.
- [ ] In the EEA (EU included): Task and dots say they're not offered; the Duet, Muse and Grok Bot pages don't mention it. Check the maker's page before paying.
