# ChatGPT dots: setup and safety checklist (facts checked 2026-11-27)

To keep a copy: paste this into Notepad and save it with the box called Save as type set to All files. On a Mac, open TextEdit, choose Format, then Make Plain Text, before you paste and save.
OpenAI changes these pages often. Before you rely on a line, open its source and check the date. Settings names are written exactly as OpenAI's pages write them.

## 1. Can I get it?
- [ ] "Dots are available in ChatGPT to Pro and selected Plus users aged 18 and older in markets excluding the European Economic Area, Switzerland, and the UK." Source: help.openai.com/en/articles/20001530-getting-started-with-your-dot
- [ ] Pro was $100 (Pro 100), $200 (Pro 200) or $500 (Pro 500) a month, USD, checked 2026-11-27. "Pro is billed monthly. Annual billing and payment for multiple months in advance are not available." Source: help.openai.com/en/articles/9793128-about-chatgpt-pro-tiers
- [ ] "Your first dot is included in your Pro or Business Premium plan at no extra cost." Source: openai.com/index/introducing-dots/
- [ ] On Plus (was $20 a month, learn.chatgpt.com/docs/pricing): "Dots are gradually rolling out to select ChatGPT Plus users". OpenAI's price table (chatgpt.com/pricing) shows Dots for Pro only. To check: "If you don't have a Dot tab, open Dots from the sidebar." Support can't switch it on for you.
- [ ] "If you change your subscription to Plus from Pro, you will lose access to your dot."
- [ ] "Dots are not yet available to users under 18." Source: help.openai.com/en/articles/20001529-dots-privacy-security-and-safety-faqs

## 2. What a dot is for
- [ ] A dot: "When you want an agent to keep track of an ongoing responsibility, figure out next steps as things change, and follow up between conversations." Source: learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots
- [ ] ChatGPT Work: "When you have a task to delegate, such as research, analysis, or creating a file, either now or on a schedule." The price table lists ChatGPT Work and Scheduled tasks on Plus too.
- [ ] "For Pro and Business Premium, conversations with your dot don't count toward your ChatGPT usage limits. Tasks your dot starts or manages in Work or Codex count toward those products' usage limits as usual."

## 3. What it can reach
- [ ] "Your dot has its own computer and browser in the cloud for research, files, and running software. Cloud work can continue while your devices are off."
- [ ] "You choose which apps dots can access" and "they can readily connect to over 4,000 apps". Source: openai.com/index/introducing-dots/
- [ ] While testing, connect a throwaway email, never your real one. (Our advice.) To make one: support.google.com/accounts/answer/27441
- [ ] "Access to your local computer is optional and starts turned off."
- [ ] Disconnecting a plugin "stops new access through that connection. It does not delete information your dot has already built into its context."

## 4. When it asks first
- [ ] "Dots start with built-in rules for when to act independently and when to ask for approval."
- [ ] "Your dot has been instructed to follow these policies: The most sensitive actions like changing a password or transferring money require you to take over so you can complete them yourself."
- [ ] Custom rules on desktop and web: "Open Settings → Personalization and select Custom rules under Permissions." Then "Select Add, describe the action, choose how your dot should handle it, and select Add rule." Custom rules are there "where available". Source: learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots/controls
- [ ] On iOS and Android: "Open the sidebar, then select Customize → Custom rules. This option appears when Behavior is unavailable."
- [ ] The four choices: "Take action without asking"; "Take action when you say so" (the help page calls it "Take action if pre-approved"); "Ask before taking action"; "Hand off to you". While testing, pick "Ask before taking action". (Our advice.)
- [ ] "Asking your dot to draft replies doesn't give it permission to send them."
- [ ] Custom rules "are instructions your dot tries to follow, and it can make mistakes." Look over its work before you rely on it.

## 5. Pause it
- [ ] "open the more options menu (•••) in its profile and select Pause . To start it again, select Paused • Tap to resume ."
- [ ] "Pause stops your dot's current main task. It doesn't stop every delegated task or cancel future scheduled runs."
- [ ] "Open a delegated task in Activity to inspect and stop that task." "Open Scheduled to disable or delete the recurring task you want to end." (Desktop app.)
- [ ] "Pausing your dot does not delete its information."

## 6. Delete it
- [ ] Profile → More options (•••) → Delete → "Select Delete to confirm or Cancel to keep your dot." "Deletion can't be undone."
- [ ] "Deleting your dot ends access to that dot and starts deletion of its own saved memories." "Your main conversation is archived".
- [ ] "Files, Codex threads, and ChatGPT conversations your dot created are stored separately. Deleting your dot does not delete them."
- [ ] "Deleting your dot doesn't undo changes already made in connected apps or recall messages already delivered to other people."
- [ ] Personal plans: the "Improve the model for everyone" setting "controls whether your dots' conversations and work may be used to improve our models."

## 7. Prompts to try (OpenAI's own examples, from learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots)
- [ ] "Help me keep our offsite on track. Read the plan and venue emails I shared, and make a list of decisions, deadlines, and things we're waiting on. Focus on anything that affects the budget or needs an answer this week. Draft suggested replies for my review, but don't send them."
- [ ] "Check the connected planning channel each weekday at 9 AM Central for the next four weeks. Update our offsite list when something changes. Message me in ChatGPT if a deadline is at risk or you need a decision from me. Keep routine updates in the checklist. Confirm the schedule."
