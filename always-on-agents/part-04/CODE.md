# Claude Duet: setup and safety checklist (facts checked 2026-11-26)

To keep a copy: paste this into Notepad and save it with the box called Save as type set to All files. On a Mac, open TextEdit, choose Format, then Make Plain Text, before you paste and save.
Claude Duet is a research preview, and Anthropic changes these pages often. Before you rely on a line, open its source and check the date. Settings names are written exactly as Anthropic's pages write them.

## 1. Can I get it?
- [ ] "To use Claude Duet, you need:" "A Pro, Max, or Ultra plan" "A Claude account in the US" "An invitation from the waitlist or from a Claude Duet user". Source: support.claude.com/en/articles/17631495
- [ ] "It isn't available on Team or Enterprise plans yet"
- [ ] Countries, two pages that disagree: the help page says "Claude Duet is available in the US only during the research preview." The product page says "Available in Pro, Max, and Ultra plans in the US, India, Indonesia, Brazil & Japan." Neither page mentions Europe or the UK. Source: claude.com/product/claude-duet
- [ ] "During the research preview, Claude Duet is included with Pro, Max, and Ultra plans at no extra cost."
- [ ] Prices when checked (2026-11-26): Pro $20 a month billed monthly, or $17 a month billed yearly ($200 up front); Max $100 a month; Ultra $200 a month. "Prices shown don't include applicable tax." Source: claude.com/pricing
- [ ] Usage: "Work it does in the background, like the morning brief and check-ins, also counts, so you may reach your limits sooner than with chat alone."
- [ ] Limit resets during the preview: "Pro: three limit resets"; "Max and Ultra: one limit reset", used in Settings, then Usage, on the web or in Claude Desktop. "Unused limit resets expire on December 15, 2026."

## 2. Join the waitlist and sign in
- [ ] "Go to the Claude Duet product page and select "Join the waitlist."" Enter your name and the email address on your Claude account, choose your plan and phone type, then select Submit.
- [ ] "Access is tied to the email address you gave us." Your phone's Apple or Google account must use the same address.
- [ ] "When it's your turn, open the invitation email on your phone and follow the setup steps for iPhone or Android."
- [ ] "Open Claude Duet and sign in with your Claude account. It can take a few minutes for the app to become available after you accept the invitation."
- [ ] On a computer: "you can open it on the web at claude.ai." Source: claude.com/product/claude-duet

## 3. Choose what it can reach
- [ ] "It works with the connectors you've connected in Claude, such as Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Slack, QuickBooks, HubSpot, Docusign, and Calendly. You start every connection yourself"
- [ ] Use a throwaway Google account made for testing, never your real inbox, until you've seen how it behaves. (Our advice.) To make one: support.google.com/accounts/answer/27441
- [ ] Sites without a connector: "Claude Duet uses its own browser in the cloud." "You can't watch Claude Duet work in its browser or take over the browser."
- [ ] Past chats: to stop Claude Duet from searching your past chats, turn off Search and reference chats in Settings, then Your Claude (or Settings, then Memory, in the older Settings layout).
- [ ] Payments: Link and Shop Pay are connected in Claude Duet: select your avatar, then payments. "Each purchase uses a one-time card that only works for that purchase." Link needs a US payment method, and "Claude Duet can't make recurring payments with Link." Sources: support.claude.com/en/articles/17666484 and 17666489
- [ ] Passwords: "When a site asks for a password, you type it into a form inside the Claude app." "Claude Duet never sees your passwords, browser cookies, or the sign-in tokens for your connected apps". Source: claude.com/resources/articles/claude-duet-safety-privacy-and-security
- [ ] "It doesn't make phone calls or send text messages to other people."

## 4. What it asks first
- [ ] Claude Duet "is designed to ask before it sends a message, makes a payment, signs a document, or deletes anything."
- [ ] "it shows you the action, the destination, and the content, including every recipient. It then waits for you to approve the action."
- [ ] "Some other steps aren't covered by a "don't ask me again" rule, however often you have approved them, like paying or sending money, deleting an order, or creating an account."
- [ ] Off-limits even with your approval: "trading securities, moving money between accounts, or accessing someone else's account."
- [ ] No Anthropic page shows an approval-settings page (checked 2026-11-26). Check each approval request yourself.
- [ ] "Claude Duet can also make mistakes, such as misreading a thread or misjudging what's urgent. Review drafts and approval requests before you confirm them."
- [ ] "It checks your accounts at set intervals, so something urgent can arrive between checks."
- [ ] Email and web pages "can contain instructions meant to mislead it. This is called prompt injection."

## 5. Disconnect it and delete what it keeps
- [ ] No Anthropic page shows a pause switch (checked 2026-11-26).
- [ ] "You can also tell it what you want to hear about and how often." Source: claude.com/resources/articles/claude-duet
- [ ] Disconnect an app in your Claude settings: "Disconnecting an app stops new access from that moment, but what Claude Duet already learned from it stays until you reset its memory, which you can do anytime from the app's settings." "switching an app off in one place switches it off in both."
- [ ] Disconnect Link or Shop Pay: select your Claude Duet avatar, select payments, choose Link or Shop Pay, then select Disconnect.
- [ ] "Claude Duet keeps your conversation history and its memory so it can carry context between tasks, until you delete your history or reset it."
- [ ] "Deleting your account wipes everything Claude Duet has stored."
