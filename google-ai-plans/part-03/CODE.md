# Do I need Google AI Ultra? A checklist (facts checked 2026-11-21, US prices)

To keep a copy: paste this into Notepad and save it with the box called Save as type set to All files. On a Mac, open TextEdit, choose Format, then Make Plain Text, before you paste and save.
Written from Google's plans page, Google One's plan comparison, the limits page and the models page, for personal Google accounts. Work and school accounts: Google says models and features depend on your organization's Workspace license. Prices and limits change: check gemini.google/us/subscriptions on the day you buy. This is a checklist, not Google's advice.

## Step 1: does Pro's limit stop you?
- [ ] On Pro, Gemini tells me I've reached my limit most weeks. (Limits "refresh every 5 hours until you reach your weekly limit".)
- [ ] What runs me out is long chats, big files or video. (Google's cost table: simple chat "Very low"; ongoing chats and large files "Medium"; Deep Research and video "High".)
- [ ] I've checked my limits: on a computer at gemini.google.com, Settings at the bottom left, then Usage limits.
No ticks? Stay on Google AI Pro (was $19.99 a month, 2026-11-21).
Only now and then? Google: "You can extend your limits by purchasing AI credits." Check that price before you jump a whole plan.

## Step 2: do I need what only Ultra has?
- [ ] I need Gemini 4 Carbon in the Gemini app: "Our strongest model designed for advanced coding and your hardest, long-horizon problems" (in the app, Ultra only; Google One also lists Carbon in AI Mode in Search for Pro).
- [ ] I need more than 5 TB of storage (Ultra 5x: 20 TB; Ultra 20x: 30 TB, Google One's table).
- [ ] I'd use more than 1,000 Google Flow credits a month (Ultra 5x: 10,000; Ultra 20x: 25,000).
- [ ] I'd use the YouTube Premium family plan (you and up to 5 household members).
No ticks in Step 1 or Step 2? Ultra isn't for you yet.

## Step 3: which Ultra tier?
Ultra 5x, was "Starting at" $99.99 a month (2026-11-21): "5x higher usage limits vs. AI Pro"
- [ ] Five times Pro's limit would be enough for me.
Ultra 20x, was $199.99 a month (2026-11-21): "20x higher usage limits vs. AI Pro"
- [ ] I'd run out even at five times Pro, or I make a lot of video in Flow, or I code all day in Google Antigravity ("Highest rate limits to agent model").
Remember: 5x and 20x are against AI Pro, not the free plan. Google publishes no daily counts for any plan.

## Step 4: moving up or down (on a computer)
- [ ] Upgrade: gemini.google.com, Settings at the bottom, the upgrade option ("Upgrade to Google AI Ultra" from Pro), then follow the on-screen instructions.
- [ ] Google doesn't say where you pick 5x or 20x: check the price on screen before you confirm.
- [ ] Step down: Google One, Manage membership, Change membership plan, a plan under "Downgrade options", Yes, continue, your payment method, Subscribe.

## Step 5: after you pay
- [ ] Pick Carbon yourself: Auto picks between Hydrogen and a fast, lightweight model. Click the model's name at the bottom of the chat box, then Gemini 4 Carbon.
- [ ] No lock icon on Carbon, and the chat box shows its name. ("Higher-tier models marked with a lock icon require an eligible plan upgrade.")
- [ ] Pick a thinking level: a model you pick yourself defaults to Low. High "carefully process[es] and reason[s] through complex, multistep prompts", and higher levels use more of your limit.

Sources: gemini.google/us/subscriptions ; one.google.com/about/google-ai-plans ; support.google.com/gemini/answer/16275805 ; support.google.com/googleone/answer/18648722 ; support.google.com/gemini/answer/14517446
