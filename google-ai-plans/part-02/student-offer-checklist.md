# Google's student offer for Gemini: signup and cancel-reminder checklist
# Facts checked 2026-11-21 on Google's own pages. Prices and dates change: check them on the day.
# To keep a copy: paste this into Notepad and save it with the box called Save as type set to All files.
# On a Mac: open TextEdit, choose Format, then Make Plain Text, before you paste and save.
# Never write your real student ID, password or card number in this file.

## 1. Do I qualify?
- [ ] I'm 18 or over.
- [ ] I'm enrolled at a college or university.
- [ ] In the United States: the offer is Google AI Pro for 1 year.
- [ ] Outside the United States: the offer is Google AI Plus in 140+ countries (not Hong Kong, Macau or Tunisia).
      Country list: https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/16548195
- [ ] I have a personal Google Account (a personal Gmail account), not my school account.
- [ ] I'm not in a family group (Google's help says I can leave it first).
- [ ] My account isn't supervised (Family Link).
- [ ] I don't get Google One through another company (a phone carrier, Google Fi, Pixel Pass, the App Store).
      If I do, Google says to cancel that plan first.

## 2. Before I start
- [ ] My age is verified on my Google Account: https://myaccount.google.com/age-verification
- [ ] I know the birthday on my Google Account (SheerID's must match it).
- [ ] My Google Play or Google Payments country matches where I'm living and studying.
- [ ] I have proof of enrollment ready, in case SheerID asks. It must clearly show:
      my full first and last name (matching what I typed in the form), my university's name or logo, and a date showing I'm enrolled now
      (for example a class schedule or a tuition receipt).

## 3. Sign up (on a computer)
- [ ] Go to https://gemini.google/students/ and click Claim Free Student Plan
      (it opens https://one.google.com/ai-student).
- [ ] Signed in to my PERSONAL Google Account.
- [ ] Select the student offer.
- [ ] Verify with SheerID. Review takes a few minutes, up to a few days at busy times.
      Don't resubmit again and again: there's a limited number of tries.
- [ ] Add a payment method (Google says a valid form of payment is required at sign-up).
- [ ] Finish the steps with the SAME account I was using when I was sent to SheerID.
- [ ] Redeemed on or before December 31, 2026.

## 4. Check it worked (our check, not Google's)
- [ ] At gemini.google.com, click Settings (at the bottom). Google's help says what the upgrade option is on each plan:
      no plan: Upgrade to Google AI Plus; Google AI Plus: Upgrade to Google AI Pro; Google AI Pro: Upgrade to Google AI Ultra.
      United States (Pro): I'm offered Ultra. Outside the United States (Plus): I'm offered Pro.

## 5. My dates
- Redeemed on: ____-__-__

- Reminder (about 11 months after I redeemed, our suggestion): ____-__-__
- Renews at (United States, was $19.99/month when checked 2026-11-21): $_____ a month
- [ ] Reminder added to my calendar.
- [ ] Each year: re-verify my student status if Google asks (eligibility must be verified each year).

## 6. Cancel before it renews (on a computer)
- [ ] gemini.google.com, Settings (at the bottom), Manage subscription.
- [ ] In Google One's settings: Cancel membership, then Cancel.
- [ ] Google's terms: if I cancel during my year, my membership MAY stay active until the year ends. Check my plan afterwards.
- [ ] Before the plan ends: am I using more than 15 GB? If so, free up space,
      or Gmail can't send or receive and nothing new can be stored.
