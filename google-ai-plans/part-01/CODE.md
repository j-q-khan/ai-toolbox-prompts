# Which Google AI plan? A checklist (facts checked 2026-11-21, US prices)

To keep a copy: paste this into Notepad and save it with the box called Save as type set to All files. On a Mac, open TextEdit, choose Format, then Make Plain Text, before you paste and save.
Written from Google's plans page, limits page and models page, for personal Google accounts. Work and school accounts: Google says models and features depend on your organization's Workspace license. Prices and limits change: check gemini.google/us/subscriptions on the day you buy. This is a checklist, not Google's advice.

## Step 1: does the Free plan stop you?
- [ ] I see a limit message most weeks.
- [ ] I want to make videos in Gemini (not on Free, per Google's feature table).
- [ ] I want the Daily Brief, scheduled actions, or to redo images with Nano Banana Pro (not on Free).
- [ ] I need more than 15 GB of storage across Gmail, Drive and Photos.
- [ ] I work with long documents (Free: 32k tokens; Plus: 128k; Pro and Ultra: 1 million).
No ticks? Stay on Free.

## Step 2: Plus or Pro?
Google AI Plus, was $4.99 a month (2026-11-21):
- [ ] I run out now and then, not every week. (Plus: "2x higher than standard limits")
- [ ] Gemini 4 Hydrogen is enough: "A high-speed, capable model that balances performance and responsiveness"
- [ ] 400 GB of storage is enough.
Google AI Pro, was $19.99 a month (2026-11-21):
- [ ] I run out most weeks. (Pro: "4x higher than standard limits")
- [ ] I want Gemini 4 Neon: "high performance for deeper analysis and complex workflows"
- [ ] I need the 1 million token window (Google: up to 1,500 pages of text or 30,000 lines of code).
- [ ] I want Gemini in Docs (Plus lists Gmail and Vids; Pro lists Gmail, Docs and Vids).
- [ ] 5 TB of storage would replace storage I already pay for.

## Step 3: do I need Ultra?
Google AI Ultra, was "Starting at" $99.99 a month, or $199.99 a month (2026-11-21):
- [ ] I already run out on Pro. ($99.99: "5x higher usage limits vs. AI Pro"; $199.99: "20x higher usage limits vs. AI Pro")
- [ ] I need Gemini 4 Carbon: "Our strongest model designed for advanced coding and your hardest, long-horizon problems"
- [ ] I'd use the YouTube Premium family plan or 20 TB or more of storage.
No ticks? Ultra isn't for you yet.

## Step 4: after you pay
- [ ] On Pro or Ultra, pick the model yourself. Google: "To use more advanced models, select them manually." Click (computer) or tap (phone app) the model's name at the bottom of the chat box.
- [ ] Pick a thinking level too: Google says that if you don't, a model you pick yourself defaults to Low.
- [ ] A lock icon on a model means it needs a higher plan.
- [ ] Change or cancel your plan: on a computer, Settings at the bottom of gemini.google.com, then Manage subscription (opens Google One settings).
- [ ] See your limits: on a computer, Settings at the bottom left, then Usage limits.
- [ ] Save usage: start a new chat when you move to a new topic (Google's tip).

## Facts to remember
- Limits are compute-based: "Your limit refreshes every 5 hours until you reach your weekly limit."
- Google publishes no daily count of chats, reports, images or videos for any plan.
- Paid plans at the limit: "your chats will continue with our fast, lightweight model."
- Every plan gets Auto: "Gemini will select Hydrogen or a fast, lightweight model".

Sources: gemini.google/us/subscriptions ; support.google.com/gemini/answer/16275805 ; support.google.com/googleone/answer/18648722 ; support.google.com/gemini/answer/14517446
