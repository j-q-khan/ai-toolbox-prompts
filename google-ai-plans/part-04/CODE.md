# Gemini or ChatGPT? A decision checklist (facts checked 2026-11-21, US prices)

To keep a copy: paste this into Notepad and save it with the box called Save as type set to All files. On a Mac, open TextEdit, choose Format, then Make Plain Text, before you paste and save.
Written from Google's and OpenAI's own pages, for personal accounts. Work and school accounts follow their organization's terms. Prices and features change: check gemini.google/us/subscriptions and learn.chatgpt.com/docs/pricing on the day you buy. This is a checklist, not either company's advice.

## Step 1: what each plan cost (was, when checked, 2026-11-21, US, per month)
Pair them by price, not by name:
| Step | Google | ChatGPT |
|---|---|---|
| Free | Free, $0 | Free, $0 |
| Smaller step | Google AI Plus, $4.99 | Go, $8 |
| About $20 | Google AI Pro, $19.99 | Plus, $20 |
| Top | Google AI Ultra, $99.99 or $199.99 | Pro, $100, $200 or $500 |
- [ ] I know Google AI Plus ($4.99) and ChatGPT Plus ($20) are different prices.
- [ ] I know Google AI Pro ($19.99) and ChatGPT Pro (from $100) are different prices.

## Step 2: where do I work all day?
- [ ] Gmail and Google Docs. (Google AI Pro's card: "Gemini in Gmail, Docs, Vids, and more". Google AI Plus's card: "Gemini in Gmail, Vids, and more".)
- [ ] Excel, Word, PowerPoint or Google Sheets. (OpenAI's table: extensions "Limited" on Free and Go, "Yes" on Plus.)
- [ ] I need more storage. (Google: 15 GB free, 400 GB Plus, 5 TB Pro, from 20 TB Ultra, across Gmail, Drive and Photos. OpenAI's pricing page lists no storage like that.)

## Step 3: do I want an agent (an assistant that does a job in steps)?
- [ ] Tasks in Gemini: "Available without a Google AI subscription in Australia, Canada, India, Japan, South Korea, and the US." 18 or over, personal account. Elsewhere: Google AI Pro or Ultra; not in the EEA, Nigeria, Switzerland or the UK.
- [ ] ChatGPT Work: "Available to all plans on desktop and to Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, and Edu on web and mobile." Free and Go: "Limited (desktop app)".
- [ ] Not the same thing: ChatGPT's "scheduled tasks" are jobs it runs at set times, like a daily update. (OpenAI's pricing table and its help page disagree on whether Free gets them; check both on the day.)

## Step 4: pictures
- [ ] Google: Nano Banana 2 on the free version; Nano Banana 2.1 "With a Google AI plan".
- [ ] OpenAI: "ChatGPT Images is available on all tiers." Free: "Limited image creation". Go: "More image creation". Plus: "More complex and accurate image creation".

## Step 5: test before you pay (one week, on a computer)
- [ ] Open both free versions: gemini.google.com and chatgpt.com.
- [ ] Give each the same three jobs I do every week:
  1. ______________________
  2. ______________________
  3. ______________________
- [ ] Note where each one stops me or asks me to wait: ______________________
- [ ] Use a temporary chat while testing. Gemini: next to New chat, click Temporary chat. ChatGPT: select Temporary (OpenAI doesn't say where it sits). Both companies say temporary chats aren't used to train their models; OpenAI says that holds "while it remains temporary", and a saved chat follows your usual setting.

## Step 6: check the training setting before I paste my own work
- [ ] ChatGPT (website): account menu, Settings, Data controls, "Improve the model for everyone". OpenAI: "On personal workspaces with ChatGPT Free, Plus, or Pro, data sharing is enabled by default."
- [ ] Gemini: the training setting is in Gemini History (myactivity.google.com/product/gemini). Google's privacy page doesn't state its starting position.
- [ ] Google's advice: "Don't enter confidential information that you wouldn't want a reviewer to see".

## Step 7: my pick
- [ ] Mostly quick questions, rarely a limit: stay free.
- [ ] Gmail and Docs, or I need storage: Google AI Pro ($19.99 when checked).
- [ ] Word, Excel or PowerPoint, or ChatGPT's agent on the web and phone: ChatGPT Plus ($20 when checked).
- [ ] Less than $20: Google AI Plus ($4.99) or ChatGPT Go ($8).
- [ ] I outgrow the $20 plans: Google AI Ultra or ChatGPT Pro (5x the price or more).
- [ ] Test one month before committing: OpenAI says there's no annual billing for Go, Plus or Pro, and Google's plans page shows monthly prices only.
