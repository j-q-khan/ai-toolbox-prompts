## Before you use a free picture
- [ ] Does the plan give a number, or only "limited"?
- [ ] Is everything I make on this plan public? (Ideogram free: yes.)
- [ ] Does the picture carry a visible mark? (Gemini: yes.)
- [ ] Do the terms allow business use, or "personal use" only? (Microsoft Copilot for individuals: personal use only.)
- [ ] Did I sign up the way that gets the free allowance? (Ideogram: Google or Apple, or link one later with the same verified email.)
- [ ] Did I save the page with the date I checked it?
- [ ] No real people in the picture.
