```
## Before you use a picture for business
- [ ] Ideogram free plan: "On the free plan, everything you generate is public." Paid plans "keep new images private until you publish them". https://docs.ideogram.ai/plans-and-pricing/available-plans
- [ ] Ideogram terms: "we do not restrict your ability to use User Output for your own purposes (including for commercial purposes)", but "We expressly disclaim any liability arising from your use of any User Output for a commercial purpose." https://ideogram.ai/legal/tos
- [ ] ChatGPT (OpenAI terms): you "(b) own the Output". https://openai.com/policies/terms-of-use/
- [ ] Gemini (Google terms): "Google won't claim ownership over that content". Gemini adds an invisible and a visible watermark: check the corners. https://policies.google.com/terms
- [ ] Firefly partner models (GPT Image, Gemini with Nano Banana, FLUX and others in the same app): "You're responsible for determining whether content generated by partner models is appropriate for your project." Check which model made your picture.
- [ ] Microsoft Copilot for individuals: "You can only use Copilot for your own personal use." https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-copilot/for-individuals/termsofuse
- [ ] FLUX (Black Forest Labs, pay as you go through the API): "Our license allows you to use model outputs commercially", and "It remains your responsibility to determine whether the outputs are appropriate for commercial use in your specific context." https://bfl.ai/licensing
- [ ] Canva and Midjourney: read their own terms; not checked for this video.
```

### 2. ai-image-prompt-template.txt
```
AI image prompt template (written for this video, 2026-11-26; works as plain words in any generator)
Fill in the four parts, then join them into one line. Delete the lines starting with #.

# 1. What's in the picture: the thing, the place, plainly
a lavender candle in a glass jar on a wooden table
# 2. Style: photo, flat drawing or watercolour, plus the light and colours
photo style, soft morning light
# 3. Format: where it will go (square for a feed post, tall for a story, wide for a banner)
square format
# 4. Words inside the picture: in quote marks, spelled exactly; or write: no text
with the words "Fresh Batch Friday" on a small card beside it
# Always add, for business pictures:
no people, no logos

Joined, ready to paste:
a lavender candle in a glass jar on a wooden table, photo style, soft morning light, square format, with the words "Fresh Batch Friday" on a small card beside it, no people, no logos

A second example:
a bakery flyer: a fresh loaf on a plain wooden shelf, flat drawing style, warm cream and brown colours, tall format for a story, with the headline "FRESH BREAD SATURDAY" at the top, no people, no logos
```
