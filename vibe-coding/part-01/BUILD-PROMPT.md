Goal: build a small tip splitter web app that runs in the browser, with no login and no database.
Steps, in order:
1. Tell me in one sentence what you'll build, and that you won't add a login, a database or any outside service.
2. Build one page with: a box for the bill amount; three tip buttons, 10%, 15% and 20%, with 15% picked to start; a box for the number of people, starting at 2, never below 1.
3. Show the tip, the total and what each person pays, updating as I type, in dollars with two decimals.
4. Add a "Round up" button that rounds each person's share up to the nearest dollar, and a way to switch rounding off again.
5. Clean, friendly design with large text and buttons. Works well on mobile.
6. Check it yourself with a $60 bill, 15% and 4 people: the tip should be $9.00, the total $69.00 and each person $17.25, or $18 rounded up. Tell me the result.
Safety: no login, no database, no tracking, no outside services and no real personal data. Keep everything else unchanged once it works.
Output: the working page, and a three-line summary of what each button does.
