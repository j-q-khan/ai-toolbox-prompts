### 2. framer-site-checklist.md
```
# Make and publish a site with Framer's agent: checklist (facts checked 2026-11-17)

To keep a copy: paste this into Notepad and save it with the box called Save as type set to All files. On a Mac, open TextEdit, choose Format, then Make Plain Text, before you paste and save.

## Is Framer the right tool?
- [ ] Framer's pages: "You can build full websites, landing pages, portfolios, and multi-page sites with CMS content."
- [ ] Need people to sign in or save their own information? That's an app: Lovable's built-in backend gives you logins and a database (Parts 2 and 3 of this series).
- [ ] Need the site's code itself, or your own hosting? Read the export section below first.

## Before your first prompt (Framer AI Notice, version 1.2, effective 2026-09-18)
- [ ] No personal data in what you type or generate (section 4.2). Made-up details only, like Test Bakery and test@example.com. For work, follow your employer's rules.
- [ ] On a non-Enterprise plan, Framer may use your inputs and outputs to train and improve its AI models unless AI model training is disabled in your Workspace settings (section 7.3). Switching it off counts going forward.
- [ ] Framer's outside model providers are not permitted to train on your inputs or outputs (section 7.1). Enterprise plans: no training (section 7.2).
- [ ] Not the same switch: "Disable AI features for this workspace" (Workspace Settings, Permissions) turns the AI features off altogether, and Framer's help page says it's Enterprise-only.

## Make the site
- [ ] Create a new site, then open the Agent tab in the right sidebar. Type in the box saying Ask Framer.
- [ ] Say what the site is for, the sections you want, and the style. A broad request like "build me a landing page" lands further from what you want.
- [ ] Optional: attach a picture as a guide (a screenshot, a mockup or a hand-drawn sketch).

## Edit it
- [ ] Change text, layout, colours, images and spacing yourself, on the canvas.
- [ ] For an agent change, select one section on the canvas first, so the agent keeps its changes to that area.
- [ ] Each message spends credits; a quick edit costs less than a big, many-step job. When credits run out, credit-using features pause until they renew, and you don't lose work.

## Publish it
- [ ] Check the words, images, layout, links and how it looks on a phone.
- [ ] Click Publish in the top right corner of the editor. Your site gets a framer.app link automatically.
- [ ] A custom domain needs a paid plan.

## Plans (was ... when checked, 2026-11-17, framer.com/pricing)
- [ ] Free plan: $0, with 500 credits to try Agents when the workspace has no active subscription. Framer: "Our Free plan is ideal for non-commercial use."
- [ ] Basic: from $10 a month, 1,000 credits a month. Pro: from $30 a month, 3,000 credits a month. Higher credit tiers cost more.
- [ ] Paid plans are Site plans, one per site; credits stack across a workspace's sites and don't roll over.
- [ ] A paid site drops Framer's branding and can use your own domain. Extra editors were $20 a month each.
- [ ] "All prices are monthly and billed according to the billing cycle selected at checkout."

## Exporting: two Framer pages disagree
- [ ] "Can I export my website to HTML and self-host it?": "Framer does not offer HTML export for self-hosting." It adds: if you need full control over hosting or the source code, "Framer may not be the right solution for that workflow."
- [ ] "Porting your data from Framer": "You can download these files at any time and move them to any hosting provider."
- [ ] If moving away matters to you, ask Framer support before you build.
- [ ] Framer's terms: "Customer retains ownership of the Content." "Customer is encouraged to archive its Content regularly and frequently."
```
