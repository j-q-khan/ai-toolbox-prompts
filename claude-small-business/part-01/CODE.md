## 1. Pick a plan
- [ ] "It's available on every paid Claude plan, and we recommend the Team plan for businesses with more than one person." (Anthropic, Sept 15, 2026)

## 2. Add the plugin
- [ ] Use Claude on the web or the Claude desktop app.
- [ ] Open the plugins page: https://claude.ai/customize/plugins
- [ ] Find Small Business and add it. The listing says "Anthropic verified".
- [ ] You may also see the plugin's marketplace page, with a button that says "Install in Cowork": https://claude.com/marketplace/plugins/small-business
- [ ] Source: https://academy.claude.com/tutorials/how-to-install-the-claude-for-small-business-plugin

## 3. Connect a tool or two, and know what each can see
- [ ] On the plugin's page, find Connectors and connect the tools you use.
- [ ] What each can see, in Anthropic's words: "If an employee can't see something in QuickBooks or Drive today, they can't see it through Claude." (May 13, 2026) and "It can't see data you don't already have access to." (install guide)
- [ ] "Microsoft 365, NetSuite, and Salesforce don't come with the plugin, so add those connectors separately."
- [ ] "On Team and Enterprise plans, an Owner may need to add a connector first."
- [ ] "No connectors? Upload a spreadsheet or statement and most workflows still run." (marketplace page)
- [ ] Try it first on a test account with made-up data, before your real books and customers.

## 4. Set it up and run your first workflow
- [ ] Type /smb-onboard, or say "set me up". It asks about your business, helps you connect your tools, and saves your context so every other skill knows it.
- [ ] To run a job: type / in the chat bar and pick it from the list, or describe the job in plain English.
- [ ] Type /monday-brief, or say "give me my Monday brief": "cash, sales, pipeline, your calendar, and the one thing that most needs you, on one page".
- [ ] Set the Monday brief to "weekly" during setup and it runs at the same time every Monday. (Anthropic, Sept 15, 2026)

## 5. Make Claude ask before money moves
- [ ] "Every workflow starts in approval mode: Claude drafts and stages the work, and nothing sends, posts, or pays until you say so." (marketplace page)
- [ ] Your permission settings (https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13345190-get-started-with-claude-cowork) and what you allow each tool to do (https://claude.com/docs/connectors/getting-started) also decide when Claude stops to ask. "Some settings let it go ahead without asking, and one of them may be your default."
- [ ] For anything that involves money or customers: check both, set Claude to ask you first, and look over each action before you approve it.
- [ ] Let a workflow run on its own only once you trust it: "You make that choice one workflow at a time and can switch it back whenever you want." (Sept 15, 2026)

## 6. Check it worked
- [ ] Confirm one line against your books.
- [ ] Pick a customer you know offhand and check that what the draft reminder says about them matches what you know.
- [ ] An invoice you know was paid but showing as open: stop and sort out the mismatch before anything goes out.

## 7. Make it yours
- [ ] Start a new chat and type: Customize the "small-business" plugin for me based on my company.

## Data
