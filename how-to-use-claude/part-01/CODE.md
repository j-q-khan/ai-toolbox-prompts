```
# Claude: first-day checklist (from Anthropic's help pages, checked 2026-11-14)

## Sign in
- [ ] Go to claude.ai and choose "Continue with Google" or "Continue with email" (you must be 18 or over, in a supported location).
- [ ] Optional: the desktop app (Windows, Mac) from claude.ai/download, or the phone app from your app store.

## Your first chat
- [ ] Type a specific prompt in the message box, the way you'd brief a coworker.
- [ ] If the answer misses, reply in the same chat with what to change ("make it shorter").
- [ ] Add a file: the "+" button (lower left), then "Add files or photos", or drag it into the chat.
- [ ] Voice mode (beta, every plan): the sound wave symbol. It counts toward your usage.

## The three settings
- [ ] Model: click its name next to the send button (top of the screen on a phone). Free: Sonnet and Haiku.
- [ ] Effort, in the same menu: Low and Medium stretch your usage further.
- [ ] Memory: Settings, then Your Claude, then "Generate memory from chats" (on by default on Free, Pro, Max, Ultra).
- [ ] Privacy: Settings, then Privacy, then the Model Improvement switch ("Help improve our AI models").
- [ ] Incognito chat: the ghost icon, top right of a new chat. Not saved to history or memory; kept 30 days.

## Before you paste
- [ ] Think twice about card or bank numbers, health information, passwords and confidential documents.
- [ ] For work: follow your employer's rules.

## One app
- [ ] Connectors: "+", then Connectors, then Manage connectors. Only connect services you trust and need.

## Check the source
- [ ] Plans and limits: claude.com/pricing (Anthropic publishes no exact message counts).
- [ ] Help pages: support.claude.com
```
