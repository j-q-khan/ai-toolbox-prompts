### 2. export-checklist.txt
```
# export-checklist.txt: before you send a Claude Slides deck
# From Anthropic's help page "What are artifacts and how do I use them?" (checked 2026-11-15).
[ ] Click through the slideshow in Claude: Present, then Present again in its menu; Right arrow or Space, Left arrow, Escape to end
[ ] In the slideshow: F turns full screen on or off; 1 to 9 jumps to that slide
[ ] Click Export and pick one: PowerPoint, PDF, or Google Slides (as editable files)
[ ] PowerPoint: if someone will keep editing it in PowerPoint
[ ] Google Slides: if your team works there
[ ] PDF: if people only need to look through it
[ ] Open the exported file yourself and click through every slide before you send it
# Deleted a slide by accident? Ctrl+Z on Windows, Cmd+Z on a Mac, brings it back.
# Need your company's PowerPoint template? The Claude for PowerPoint add-in works inside PowerPoint
# (Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise, checked 2026-11-15 on claude.com/docs/office-agents/powerpoint).
```
