### 2. before-commercial-use-checklist.txt

# before-commercial-use-checklist.txt: before you use a Claude Design logo in business
# Built only from official pages, checked 2026-11-15. This is not legal advice.
# Sources: anthropic.com/legal/consumer-terms, anthropic.com/legal/commercial-terms,

# "Copyright and Artificial Intelligence, Part 2: Copyrightability" (January 2025).
[ ] Which terms cover you? Free, Pro, Max, Ultra: Consumer Terms. Team, Enterprise: Commercial Terms.
[ ] Consumer Terms, section 4: "we assign to you all our right, title, and interest (if any) in Outputs."
[ ] Commercial Terms, section B: Customer "owns its Outputs"; Anthropic assigns "its right, title and interest (if any)".
[ ] Consumer Terms, section 3: you may not use the services "To infringe, misappropriate, or violate intellectual property or other legal rights".
[ ] Consumer Terms, section 4: "You should not rely on any Outputs or Actions without independently confirming their accuracy."
[ ] Copyright Office: "Copyright does not extend to purely AI-generated material, or material where there is insufficient human control over the expressive elements."
[ ] Copyright Office: "Based on the functioning of current generally available technology, prompts do not alone provide sufficient control."
[ ] Copyright Office: your own "creative selection, coordination, or arrangement" or "creative modifications" can count; whether it is enough "must be analyzed on a case-by-case basis".
[ ] Trademark is a different law. The Copyright Office report doesn't cover it. Check trademark separately.
[ ] Check with a lawyer for your situation before you use the logo in business.
# Brand files you upload: on Free, Pro, Max and Ultra, chats can be used to improve Claude if you allow it (the Model Improvement setting, privacy.claude.com); at work, follow your employer's rules.
