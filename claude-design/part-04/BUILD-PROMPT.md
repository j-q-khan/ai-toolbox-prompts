Goal: mock up a draft landing page and a clickable booking screen in Claude Design from my brief, plus a short handoff note for Claude Code.
My brief (made up; Pebble Lane Bakery is not a real business; replace it with your own non-confidential brief):
- Page: a landing page that signs people up for the Pebble Lane Bakery Saturday bread class.
- Who it's for: local beginners who have never baked bread.
- Layout: a big heading, one short line under it, the four dates, the price, and one button that says Book a place.
- On the page: four Saturday mornings, 10:00 to 13:00, 25 dollars a place, flour and aprons included.
- Phones: it must work on a phone as well as a laptop.
Steps, in order:
1. If anything in my brief is unclear, ask me up to two questions first, and wait for my answers.
2. Make the landing page as one artboard: the heading, one short line, the four dates, the price and one Book a place button.
3. Make a second artboard, a booking screen with the four dates and a name and email form, and link the Book a place button to it so I can click through.
4. Use only the facts in my brief. Don't invent prices, dates, reviews, names or results.
5. Put the word "Draft" on every screen. The form is for show only and sends nothing; use hello@pebblelane.example wherever an email address is needed.
6. Use plain shapes and colours only: no real company's logo, brand or photos of real people.
7. When the design is done, write a handoff note of five lines at most: what each screen is, what the button does, and that it must work on phones, so I can pass the design on from Export, then Handoff to Claude Code.
8. Remind me in one line that Export also has Export as standalone HTML.
Output: two screens marked as drafts, and the handoff note.
