# Claude build prompt: Claude Design Tutorial for Beginners: Your First Design in Claude (2026)

Part 1 of the Claude Design series. Copy everything below the line into a new chat with Claude.
It follows Anthropic's official Claude Design help pages (checked 2026-11-15). Use only made-up or your own non-confidential details, never a client's or employer's files, and check everything Claude makes before you rely on it.

---

Paste this into a new chat with Claude (any plan, Free included), after turning on Settings, then Capabilities, then Create and edit files. Use the made-up brief below, never a client's or employer's files.

Goal: make a one-page design (a one-pager) for a made-up bakery's bread class, so I can practise Claude Design from a written brief.
Brief (made up; Pebble Lane Bakery is not a real business):
- What: Weekend Bread Class at Pebble Lane Bakery
- When: Saturday 10:00 to 13:00
- Price: 25 dollars, flour and aprons included
- How to sign up: at the counter
- Audience: neighbours who've never baked bread
Steps, in order:
1. Before you design anything, ask me up to three short questions if anything in the brief is unclear, and wait for my answers.
2. Make the one-pager as a Claude Design artifact: a big heading, the date and price in a box, three short reasons to come, and a sign-up line at the bottom.
3. Use only the facts in the brief. Don't invent prices, dates, addresses, phone numbers, reviews, quotes or people. Where something is missing, write a clear placeholder such as [ADD ADDRESS].
4. Don't use or imitate any real company's name, logo, colours or fonts, and draw no logo; write the bakery's name in plain text.
5. Put the word DRAFT in the top corner of the page.
6. When it's done, tell me in two sentences what you made, and list the three ways I can change it: chat, an inline comment on one spot, or editing on the canvas.
7. Offer me two other layouts to compare, and don't change the first one until I pick.
Output: one one-pager design marked DRAFT, built only from the brief above.
