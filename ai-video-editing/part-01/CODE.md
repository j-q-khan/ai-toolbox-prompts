```
# The best AI video editor for each job: checklist (facts checked 2026-11-19)

To keep a copy: paste this into Notepad and save it with the box called Save as type set to All files, as ai-editing-jobs-checklist.md. On a Mac: open TextEdit, choose Format, then Make Plain Text, before you paste and save.

Every fact below is from the company's own page on 2026-11-19. Nothing was signed up for, installed or run. Prices and plans change: check each page before you pay.

## Captions (auto captions = subtitles the app writes by listening to your voice)
- [ ] Adobe Premiere (Premiere Pro is now called Adobe Premiere): Speech to Text, captions, subtitles and transcripts in 18 languages, included in the Premiere subscription. Text panel, Transcribe Sequence, then Create Captions. https://www.adobe.com/products/premiere/speech-to-text.html
- [ ] Shortcut: turn on Automatic transcription in Premiere's Import settings.
- [ ] CapCut: Auto Captions, also called Recognise Subtitles, on web, desktop and mobile. Phone: Captions in the bottom menu, then Auto Captions. https://www.capcut.com/help/how-to-recognise-subtitles
- [ ] Check names and odd words before you post.

## Filler words (the ums, uhs and you-knows)
- [ ] Descript: AI Tools panel in the right sidebar, then Remove filler words. English transcripts only. Uses AI credits. https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10164806394509-Remove-filler-words

## Long video to shorts (a clip maker)
- [ ] OpusClip: "turns long videos into high-quality viral clips". https://www.opus.pro/
- [ ] Riverside: Magic Clips. https://riverside.com/magic-clips
- [ ] CapCut's own clip maker: see Part 2 of this series.

## Vertical (aspect ratio 9:16) and reframing
- [ ] Adobe Premiere: Auto Reframe. https://www.adobe.com/products/premiere/ai-video-editing.html
- [ ] CapCut: Auto Reframe, "available in specific regions". https://www.capcut.com/tools/auto-reframe

## A noisy voice
- [ ] Adobe Premiere: Enhance Speech, included with the membership (Adobe's assistive AI). https://www.adobe.com/products/premiere/ai-video-editing.html

## Finding one shot
- [ ] Adobe Premiere: the Search panel finds clips you describe in everyday language.

## DaVinci Resolve (Blackmagic Design)
- [ ] The free download lists no AI tools; text-based editing, AI Animated Subtitles, smart reframe and the other AI tools are listed for DaVinci Resolve Studio, the paid version.
- [ ] Studio was $236 (down from $295) when checked on 2026-11-19. https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve/studio

## Credits, generative AI and watermarks
- [ ] Credits: an allowance that comes with your plan; some features spend them each time.
- [ ] Premiere's Generative Extend: "a premium generative AI feature" that needs generative credits after a limited number of complimentary generations; up to 2 seconds of video, 10 seconds of audio.
- [ ] The Premiere plan was US$22.99 a month (annual, billed monthly) with 25 generative credits a month when checked on 2026-11-19. https://www.adobe.com/products/premiere/plans.html?country=US
- [ ] OpusClip Free: "Post one clip every day for free"; animated captions "With watermark"; 3-day export limit. https://www.opus.pro/pricing
- [ ] Descript Free: 60 media minutes a month, 100 AI credits one-time, 720p, watermark-free export not included. https://www.descript.com/pricing
- [ ] Riverside: "Magic Clips is free". Its pricing page showed a staging notice on 2026-11-19, so check it yourself: https://riverside.com/pricing

## Free, in the companies' own words
- [ ] Adobe's Premiere phone app: "free, standalone video editing mobile app", "free unlimited captions", no watermarks, iOS and Android. https://www.adobe.com/products/premiere/app.html
- [ ] CapCut Web: "the AI caption feature on CapCut Web is free to use without any hidden costs". But CapCut's pricing page lists "Basic editing tools" for Free and "Access to AI features" for Pro, and says "Plan availability and features may vary by region, platform, and current offering." https://www.capcut.com/pricing
```
