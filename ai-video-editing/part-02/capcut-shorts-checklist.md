# CapCut shorts checklist: captions, Auto Cut, clip maker (facts checked 2026-11-19)

To keep a copy: paste this into Notepad and save it with the box called Save as type set to All files, as capcut-shorts-checklist.md. On a Mac: open TextEdit, choose Format, then Make Plain Text, before you paste and save.

Every fact below is from CapCut's own pages on 2026-11-19. Nothing was signed up for, installed or run. Features, plans and regions change: check CapCut's pages before you count on one.

## 1. Auto captions (help name "Recognise Subtitles")
- [ ] Phone: open your video in a new edit, tap Captions in the bottom menu, then Auto Captions, confirm the language, tap Generate.
- [ ] Computer: Captions in the left toolbar (speech-bubble icon), then Auto Captions, then Generate.
- [ ] CapCut Web: Captions on the left panel, then Auto Captions, then Recognise, choose the language, then Generate.
- [ ] Tap any subtitle box to fix its text, timing or look. CapCut: "always review auto-generated subtitles and make manual corrections where needed".
- [ ] Already have a caption file (.srt)? CapCut Web: Captions section, import captions.
https://www.capcut.com/help/how-to-recognise-subtitles

## 2. Auto Cut (phone and computer apps only)
- [ ] Phone: tap the Templates icon (top right), then AI Creation, then look for Auto Cut, Beat Cut or Smart Edit.
- [ ] Start a fresh edit with your own clips (it may not appear in a downloaded template).
- [ ] Internet on: it works online.
- [ ] Version: CapCut 12.0 or later on the phone, 5.0 or later on a computer. Not on CapCut Web.
- [ ] Still missing? CapCut: "Auto Cut is still in phased or regional rollout"; it may not be enabled for your account yet.
https://www.capcut.com/help/auto-cut-not-working

## 3. Long video to shorts (AI Clip Maker)
- [ ] Upload the long video (from your computer, Google Drive or Dropbox) and keep the window open while it uploads.
- [ ] Preview each clip, Edit more to change one, Export to save it.
- [ ] Check each clip on its own: it must make sense without the rest of the video.
https://www.capcut.com/tools/long-video-to-shorts

## 4. Vertical (aspect ratio 9:16) with Auto Reframe (computer app)
- [ ] Select the video in the timeline, Video section in the right-hand panel, turn on Auto reframe, pick the Aspect ratio, click Apply.
- [ ] CapCut: "Note: this feature is available in specific regions."
https://www.capcut.com/tools/auto-reframe

## 5. A clearer voice (computer app)
- [ ] Right editing panel, Basic, Enhance quality: set anywhere from 0% to 100%, then listen back.
https://www.capcut.com/tools/voice-enhancer

## 6. Free or Pro, in CapCut's own words
- [ ] Pricing page: Free lists "Free templates to start fast" and "Basic editing tools"; Pro lists "Access to AI features" and "650 monthly AI credits". Prices are "for reference only" and vary by region. https://www.capcut.com/pricing
- [ ] AI caption page: "the AI caption feature on CapCut Web is free to use without any hidden costs". https://www.capcut.com/tools/ai-caption-generator
- [ ] Diamond icons: blue items are for Standard plan users; "Features marked with the purple diamond icon belong to the Pro plan." https://www.capcut.com/help/standard-version-access-to-diamond-items
- [ ] Credits (an allowance that comes with a paid plan): CapCut's credit rules list 2 features that spend them for now (Motion effects, AI effects). https://www.capcut.com/clause/credits-rule
- [ ] Terms: "The Services, in full or in part, may not be available in all jurisdictions". https://www.capcut.com/clause/terms-of-service

## 7. Before you post each short
- [ ] Every caption checked against what's said, names spelled right.
- [ ] Tall (9:16), main subject in frame.
- [ ] Makes sense on its own.
- [ ] Voice clear with the sound up; captions carry it with the sound off.
