# AI Music, Part 3: AI Music Video: Turn Your Song Into a Music Video With Suno Visuals

Free files for the video. Facts checked 2026-11-26; Suno and YouTube change often, so open each source before you rely on a line.

- `shot-list-planner.txt`: the shot list from the video for "Bench at Nine" (the example song from Part 2), one line per clip of 5 to 15 seconds, plus a blank template for your own song. Every character is made up; no real people.
- `youtube-posting-checklist.md`: Visual credits in Suno's words, the watermark, rights, and YouTube's disclosure, inauthentic-content and Content ID pages, each line with its source.
- `BUILD-PROMPT.md`: paste it into Claude Code in an empty folder to build a local page that splits your song's sections into clips of 5 to 15 seconds and shows the posting checklist. It downloads nothing and runs no third-party code.
- `CODE.md`: both files above in one place, exactly as shown in the video.
- `SOURCES.md`: every page the facts come from, with the date it was checked.

Nothing in this folder was generated with any music or video tool, and no song or video was made for the video. This is not legal advice. Not affiliated with Suno, Udio, Google, ElevenLabs or YouTube.
