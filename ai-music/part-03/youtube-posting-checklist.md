# Posting an AI music video on YouTube: checklist (facts checked 2026-11-26)

To keep a copy: paste this into Notepad and save it with the box called Save as type set to All files. On a Mac, open TextEdit, choose Format, then Make Plain Text, before you paste and save.
Suno and YouTube change often. Before you rely on a line, open its source and check the date. This is not legal advice.

## Before you make it (Suno Visuals)
- [ ] Visual credits: "Anybody can buy Visuals credits." "For a limited time, Pro and Premier subscribers will have a limited number of Visual credits to try out the product." Source: help.suno.com/en/articles/14661249
- [ ] Platinum lists "20,000 visual credits a month". Source: suno.com/pricing
- [ ] Cost per clip isn't printed: "Credit cost for a Visual is determined by a few variables including the duration (for videos) and quality you select." "Fast costs less Visual credits than Standard." Source: help.suno.com/en/articles/14661249
- [ ] Clips are 5 to 15 seconds. Song menu, then Create Visual, then Video Clip. Source: help.suno.com/en/articles/14662785
- [ ] A made-up character only. Suno's filters block prompts that try to show specific artists, public figures and copyrighted works. Source: suno.com/blog/introducing-visuals

## Before it leaves Suno
- [ ] Watermark left alone: Suno will "apply embedded watermarks to Visuals before they can be shared off-platform", and its terms say "You agree not to remove, alter, obscure or circumvent any fingerprint, watermark or metadata Suno appends to an Output". Sources: suno.com/blog/introducing-visuals ; suno.com/terms
- [ ] Rights: Suno's terms cover "audio and visual output", but no Suno page spells out commercial use for Visuals clips. Read the terms before you monetise. Source: suno.com/terms
- [ ] Free-plan songs: "These songs cannot be monetized". Source: help.suno.com/en/articles/9601601

## When you upload (YouTube Studio)
- [ ] Disclosure: "We require creators to disclose AI-generated or altered content that seems realistic." The list includes "Creates music that's the main focus of the video" and "AI generated music". Set "AI use" under Attributes to Yes. Source: support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491
- [ ] Monetisation, inauthentic content (updated July 15, 2025). Not allowed: "AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates giving the impression of mass production"; "Image slideshows, templated storylines, or scrolling text with minimal or no narrative"; "videos that stitch together unrelated or inconsistent AI clips to surprise or shock viewers". Allowed: "using AI to visualize a unique character and narrative you invented"; "the final product must still demonstrate your creative vision". Source: support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392
- [ ] Content ID: "When a video is uploaded to YouTube, it's automatically scanned by Content ID." No YouTube page we found says how Content ID treats AI music. Source: support.google.com/youtube/answer/2797370
- [ ] Copyright (U.S. only, may change): protection where a human contributes, "but not the mere provision of prompts". Source: copyright.gov/newsnet/2025/1060.html
