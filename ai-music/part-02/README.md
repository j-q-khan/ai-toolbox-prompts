# AI Music, Part 2: AI Music With Suno: Make Your First AI Song With Your Own Lyrics (Suno v6 Tutorial)

Free files for the video. Facts checked 2026-11-25; Suno changes often, so open each source before you rely on a line.

- `lyric-template.txt`: the song from the video, "Bench at Nine" (an original style prompt and ten lines of lyrics written for it, with [Verse], [Chorus] and [Outro] labels), plus a blank template for your own song. Genre, instruments, mood and voice only; no artist names.
- `style-prompt-checklist.md`: what to put in Suno's Styles box and lyrics box, what Create costs, and what the free plan lets you do with the song, each line with its date and a source.
- `BUILD-PROMPT.md`: paste it into Claude Code in an empty folder to build a local lyric-sheet page that lays out your lyrics with their labels and checks your style prompt for artist names you list. It downloads nothing and runs no third-party code.
- `CODE.md`: both files above in one place, exactly as shown in the video.
- `SOURCES.md`: every page the facts come from, with the date it was checked.

Nothing in this folder was generated with any music tool, and no song was made for the video. This is not legal advice. Not affiliated with Suno, Udio, Google, ElevenLabs or YouTube.
