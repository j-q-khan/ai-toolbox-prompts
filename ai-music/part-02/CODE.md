### 2. style-prompt-checklist.md
```
# Suno style prompt checklist (facts checked 2026-11-25)

To keep a copy: paste this into Notepad and save it with the box called Save as type set to All files. On a Mac, open TextEdit, choose Format, then Make Plain Text, before you paste and save.
Suno changes often. Before you rely on a line, open its source and check the date. This is not legal advice.

## Your style prompt (the Styles box)
- [ ] A genre: for example indie pop, folk, synth pop, soul, lo-fi hip hop.
- [ ] Two or three instruments: for example bright electric guitar, piano, handclaps, steady drums.
- [ ] A mood: for example hopeful, calm, late-night, triumphant.
- [ ] A voice: for example warm male vocal, soft female vocal, choir, no vocals.
- [ ] No artist names, band names or song titles. Suno's help: "your songs may not generate if they include: The names of well known artists or people". Source: help.suno.com/en/articles/3198209
- [ ] Want Suno to stick to your style tags? The v6 FAQ: "If you'd like to retain full control of your style tags, reduce the Variety slider to 0." Source: help.suno.com/en/articles/13924481

## Your lyrics (the lyrics box)
- [ ] Your own words. Suno: "you retain ownership and rights to any original lyrics you input into Suno." Source: help.suno.com/en/articles/2415873
- [ ] A label above each part: [Verse], [Chorus], [Outro] (the square brackets are our habit; Suno's notes show no format). Suno's release notes (July 9, 2026): "Add labels like "Verse" and "Outro" to organize your lyrics". Source: suno.com/release-notes
- [ ] Never someone else's lyrics. Suno: "Don't try to monetize a song if you're using someone else's lyrics!" Source: help.suno.com/en/articles/2410177

## Making it (Create, then Custom)
- [ ] Title typed, so you can tell versions apart.
- [ ] Model (top right of the Create form): v6-mini on Free; v6 and v6-wild on Pro and Premier. Source: help.suno.com/en/articles/13924801
- [ ] Credits: "Each time you generate, you make two songs with a total cost of 10 credits." Free: "50 song credits per day". Sources: help.suno.com/en/articles/13924481 ; suno.com/pricing
- [ ] Play both songs. Keep the take that gets the most right.
- [ ] To change the words: three-dot menu, then Reuse Prompt. Source: help.suno.com/en/articles/2417409
- [ ] To redo one part: Replace Section. The pricing page lists "Advanced editing (replace or add section)" on Pro and up, not Free; "Remix songs (extend, cover, adjust speed)" on every plan. Source: suno.com/pricing

## Before it earns money (checked 2026-11-25)
- [ ] Free plan: "No monthly song downloads", "No commercial rights". Free songs "cannot be monetized". Source: suno.com/pricing ; help.suno.com/en/articles/9601601
- [ ] Trial downloads: "Trial downloads are not eligible for commercial use." Source: help.suno.com/en/articles/13876865
- [ ] Ownership: "If you make music with the Basic (free) plan, Suno is the owner of the songs." "If you make songs while subscribed to the Pro or Premier plan, you own the songs." Source: help.suno.com/en/articles/2746945
- [ ] Pro was $10 a month, or $8 a month billed yearly ($96/year), when checked, 2026-11-25: 20 song downloads a month, commercial use rights. Source: suno.com/pricing
- [ ] Pick the plan first: "starting a subscription after you made a great song will not give you a retroactive license for the song." Source: help.suno.com/en/articles/2410177
```
