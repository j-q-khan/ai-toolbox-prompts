### 2. free-plan-checklist.md
```
# Free AI music plans: what each one really gives you (facts checked 2026-11-25)

To keep a copy: paste this into Notepad and save it with the box called Save as type set to All files. On a Mac, open TextEdit, choose Format, then Make Plain Text, before you paste and save.
Plans change. Before you rely on a row, open its source and check the date. This is not legal advice.

## Before you make a song that matters
- [ ] Decide what it's for: listening and sharing, or earning money (a monetised video, an ad, a track you sell).
- [ ] If it might earn money, pick the plan first. Suno: "starting a subscription after you made a great song will not give you a retroactive license for the song." (help.suno.com/en/articles/2410177, 2026-11-25)
- [ ] Write your own lyrics. Suno: "If you wrote the lyrics for your song(s), you own those lyrics." (help.suno.com/en/articles/2746945, 2026-11-25)
- [ ] Style prompt: genres, moods, instruments and voice only. No artist names.

## Suno Free (checked 2026-11-25)
- [ ] Credits: "50 song credits per day", on the free models (v6-mini). Source: suno.com/pricing
- [ ] Downloads: "No monthly song downloads". Free accounts may get trial downloads; "Trial downloads are not eligible for commercial use." Source: help.suno.com/en/articles/13876865
- [ ] Commercial use: "No commercial rights". Free songs are "only intended for personal, non-commercial use" and "cannot be monetized". Source: help.suno.com/en/articles/9601601
- [ ] Ownership: "If you make music with the Basic (free) plan, Suno is the owner of the songs." Source: help.suno.com/en/articles/2746945
- [ ] Pro was $10 a month, or $8 a month billed yearly ($96/year), when checked, 2026-11-25: 20 song downloads a month, commercial use rights. Source: suno.com/pricing

## Udio (checked 2026-11-25)
- [ ] Downloads: "downloading of audio, video, and stems has been disabled" (help page dated February 17, 2026). Source: help.udio.com/articles/12683565
- [ ] Free credits, prices and terms: Udio's pricing and terms pages need JavaScript and didn't load for us. Not quoted.

## Gemini with Lyria (checked 2026-11-25)
- [ ] You need: 18 or older, a personal Google account (or a supported work or school account), Gemini history on. Source: support.google.com/gemini/answer/16901237
- [ ] Steps: Upload & tools, then Create music. Download at the top right of the track image: MP4 with cover art, or MP3.
- [ ] Watermark: "Each generated track will have an embedded digital watermark to help identify it as AI-generated."
- [ ] Limits: "There are limits for how many tracks you can create with Lyria." Free numbers: not printed on the help page.
- [ ] Ownership: "Google won't claim ownership over that content." Source: policies.google.com/terms
- [ ] Commercial use: no clause found on 2026-11-25. Check Google's terms before you monetise.

## ACE-Step 1.5 (checked 2026-11-25)
- [ ] Code licence: MIT. Latest release v0.1.8 (2026-05-18). Source: github.com/ace-step/ACE-Step-1.5
- [ ] Model files' licence: not stated on the README. Check it before commercial use.
- [ ] Hardware: "The model runs locally with less than 4GB of VRAM"; "CUDA GPU recommended". Its XL models need 12GB or more.
- [ ] Setup (README Quick Start): Python 3.11 or 3.12, install uv, copy the project, start it; it opens in your web browser. Windows and Mac portable packages exist.
- [ ] Safety: it runs someone else's code on your computer. Use a test folder.
- [ ] The README asks users to "clearly disclose AI involvement" and "verify the originality of generated works".

## ElevenLabs (checked 2026-11-25)
- [ ] Its music page says Free is "Personal use only"; its music terms (last updated 09 October 2026) list Free commercial use as permitted, with downloads "Not permitted". The pages disagree: don't rely on free ElevenLabs music for commercial use. Sources: elevenlabs.io/music ; elevenlabs.io/eleven-music-model-specific-terms

## Copyright
- [ ] Suno: "the material may not be eligible for copyright protection." and "Writing the prompt does not constitute the creation of the song." In the US it's decided case by case, and it may change.
```
