It drafts the opt-in wording, the first automatic reply and the hand-over-to-a-person message for a WhatsApp chatbot, plus a fallback reply and an opt-out reply. Try it first with the made-up Test Bakery details in the prompt, then swap in your own. Don't paste anything private: no passwords, customer names or phone numbers. Check every line against your real business and against Meta's current WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy before you use it. Written from the makers' docs, not run.

I run a small business and I'm setting up a chatbot that answers customers on WhatsApp. Write the messages below for it, using only the details I give you.

[YOUR DETAILS. To try it first, use this made-up example: Business name: Test Bakery. Opening hours: Tuesday to Saturday, 8am to 4pm. What the chatbot may answer: opening hours, our cakes and prices, delivery within 5 miles, how to order. How to reach a person: call +1 555 0100 or email test@example.com, Tuesday to Saturday, 8am to 4pm. Where customers sign up: a tick box on our website's order form.]

Write these five things:
1. OPT-IN WORDING, under 40 words, for the place where customers sign up. It must clearly say they are agreeing to get WhatsApp messages from the business, name the business, say what kind of messages they'll get, and say how to stop (for example, reply STOP).
2. FIRST AUTOMATIC REPLY, under 40 words. It must say it's an AI assistant, not a person, say it answers questions about this business only, and say how to reach a person.
3. HAND-OVER MESSAGE, under 30 words, for when the customer asks for a person or the question is outside what the chatbot may answer: it says a person will take over, and when they're available.
4. FALLBACK REPLY, under 30 words, for when the chatbot doesn't know: it says so plainly and gives the way to reach a person.
5. OPT-OUT REPLY, under 25 words, for when someone replies STOP: it confirms they won't get more messages.
Rules: never invent prices, hours, addresses or policies; if my details don't say it, leave it out. Each message must fit in one WhatsApp message, not several. Use plain, friendly English. After the five messages, list anything I should add to my details to make them complete.
