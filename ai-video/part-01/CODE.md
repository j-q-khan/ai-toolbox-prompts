### 1. ai-video-jobs-checklist.md

# The best AI video generator for each job: checklist (facts checked 2026-11-23)

To keep a copy: paste this into Notepad and save it with the box called Save as type set to All files, as ai-video-jobs-checklist.md. On a Mac: open TextEdit, choose Format, then Make Plain Text, before you paste and save.

Every fact below is from the maker's own page on 2026-11-23. Nothing was signed up for, installed, run or generated. Prices, plans and models change: check each page before you pay.

## The four words
- [ ] Prompt: the description you type (what's in the shot, how it moves, how it should look).
- [ ] Text to video: the whole clip from your prompt alone.
- [ ] Image to video: a photo plus your prompt; the photo is the starting point.
- [ ] Credits: the allowance your plan gives you; every clip spends some. Make short test clips first.

## Long clips with sound: Kling
- [ ] Kling 4.0: 3 to 30 second clips, two-channel stereo audio; Kling's user guide lists 720p, 1080p and 4K. https://kling.ai/quickstart/kling-4-model-user-guide
- [ ] Kling's free Basic plan: "generated content is not for commercial use"; "Free tier includes watermark". https://kling.ai/blog/kling-video-3-0-credit-cost-guide
- [ ] Also up to 30 seconds: Seedance 2.5 on Dreamina (ByteDance), rolling out to subscribers over 16 in Europe, Asia, the Middle East and South America. https://dreamina.capcut.com/resource/seedance-2-5-launch

## Try one with no subscription: Google Flow
- [ ] Flow: "With no subscription, you receive only 50 Google Flow credits per day to try Google Flow." https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16526234
- [ ] Omni Flash clips: 3 to 10 seconds without a subscription, 3 to 15 with Google AI Plus, Pro or Ultra. https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16352836
- [ ] Before you generate: click Settings at the top right of the prompt box to check the model, resolution and latest credit costs.
- [ ] Veo 3.1 Fast, Veo 3.1 Quality and Omni Flash 1.1 retire on December 5, 2026; Google recommends Omni Flash 1.2 or Veo 3.1 Lite.
- [ ] Gemini app: "Gemini Omni will replace Veo in the Gemini app"; 10 second videos; "Google AI subscription required". https://gemini.google/us/overview/video-generation/?hl=en

## Selling your clips: Runway
- [ ] Runway's Terms of Use (Oct 23, 2026): "the Company does not restrict your commercial use of your Outputs" (no plan named). https://runway.com/terms-of-use
- [ ] Free plan: "125 one-time credits to explore Runway's AI tools" (once, not every month). Gen-4.5: 12 credits per second. Standard was $15 a month ($12 billed yearly) on 2026-11-23. https://runway.com/pricing
- [ ] Never use another person's image, video or audio without their permission (Runway's Usage Policy). https://runway.com/safety/usage-policy

## Steering each moment: Luma
- [ ] Ray3.2: up to 16 keyframes in a single clip; clips up to 20 seconds at 1080p. https://lumalabs.ai/news/introducing-ray-3-2
- [ ] Commercial use only for clips made during an active paid subscription. Plus was $30 a month ($25 billed yearly) on 2026-11-23. https://lumalabs.ai/legal/terms-of-service

## Mixing ingredients: Hailuo (MiniMax H3)
- [ ] MiniMax H3 takes text, images, video and audio together; clips up to 15 seconds at 2K. https://www.minimax.io/blog/minimax-h3
- [ ] Free downloads include a watermark (Hailuo's subscription terms). https://hailuoai.video/doc/payment-policy.html

## Not on the list
- [ ] Sora: "The Sora web and app experiences were discontinued on April 26, 2026." https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001152-what-to-know-about-the-sora-discontinuation

## Was a clip made with AI? Three checks, and their limits
- [ ] A visible watermark (Kling free tier, Hailuo free downloads). Paid plans often drop it, so a clean video proves nothing. Luma's and Hailuo's terms forbid removing one.
- [ ] SynthID (every Gemini Omni video): gemini.google.com, signed in with your Google account, Add files, a video under 90 seconds, ask if it was made with AI. "SynthID not detected" means not made by Google AI, but "it could have been created by other AI systems". https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16722517
- [ ] Content Credentials (Runway's own models on every plan; Seedance 2.5): signed data that travels with the file, but "they can be separated", and stripped metadata means they're "lost forever". https://c2pa.org/faqs/
- [ ] Posting to YouTube: answer the "AI use" question in YouTube Studio; yes for a realistic scene that didn't happen or a real person saying something they didn't say. https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491
```

### 2. text-to-video-prompt-template.txt

# A text to video prompt template, written for this video (2026-11-23). Not run: nothing was generated.
# To keep a copy: paste this into Notepad and save it as text-to-video-prompt-template.txt with the box called Save as type set to All files.
# On a Mac: open TextEdit, choose Format, then Make Plain Text, before you paste and save.
# Fill in each line, then paste the finished prompt into any generator as one line.
# Keep real people and faces out: use products, places and scenes.

Shot: [wide / close-up / medium], [slow push in / slow pan / handheld drift]
Subject: [what's in the shot, e.g. a steaming coffee cup on a wooden table]
Motion: [one gentle movement, e.g. steam rising, waves breaking, clouds drifting]
Style: [light and mood, e.g. soft morning light, golden hour, calm]
Sound: [one ambient sound, e.g. a quiet cafe, waves and gulls]
Length: [3 to 30 seconds; start with a short test clip]

# Example 1 (product):
# Shot: close-up, slow push in. Subject: a steaming coffee cup on a wooden table. Motion: steam rising. Style: soft morning light. Sound: a quiet cafe. Length: 6 seconds.
# Example 2 (place):
# Shot: wide, slow push in. Subject: a lighthouse on a rocky coast. Motion: waves breaking, clouds drifting. Style: golden hour, calm. Sound: waves and gulls. Length: 8 seconds.
```
