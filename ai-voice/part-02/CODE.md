# ElevenLabs voice cloning: recording checklist (facts checked 2026-11-26)

To keep a copy: paste this into Notepad and save it with the box called Save as type set to All files. On a Mac, open TextEdit, choose Format, then Make Plain Text, before you paste and save.
Plans and docs change. Before you rely on a line, open its source and check the date. This is not legal advice.

## Whose voice
- [ ] It's my own voice. (ElevenLabs' Professional Voice Clone: "Even with their consent, you cannot clone someone else's voice.")
- [ ] Or, for an Instant Voice Clone only: another adult has given "explicit and informed consent" (use policy), and I've filled in consent-note-template.txt with them.
- [ ] Nobody under 18. Use policy: "You may not submit voice recordings of anyone under 18 to create or train a voice model."
- [ ] Not a public figure. ElevenLabs' no-go voices safeguard "restricts the creation of voice clones that approximate the voices of prominent public figures".

## The room and the microphone (ElevenLabs' cloning pages)
- [ ] One speaker only, with no music, echo or background noise of any kind.
- [ ] A quiet room. If it echoes, a thick duvet or quilt can soften it.
- [ ] About two fists away from the microphone. "Any microphone will work", and "High-quality input = high-quality output."
- [ ] A pop filter, if I have one ("This will minimize plosives").
- [ ] A steady volume: loud enough to be clear, not so loud it distorts. The docs' ideal: between -23 dB and -18 dB RMS, with a true peak of -3 dB.

## The performance
- [ ] One steady tone, start to finish. The clone copies how I talk: "If you talk in a slow, monotone voice without much emotion, that is what the AI will mimic."
- [ ] The style I want my voiceovers in (calm for tutorials, for example).
- [ ] Long pauses and "um"s cut out first, because the clone copies those too.

## Length and file type
- [ ] Instant Voice Clone: about 1-2 minutes in total; avoid more than 3. Each file at least 5 seconds; all files at least 10 seconds.
- [ ] Professional Voice Clone: the page gives several figures; "The bare minimum we recommend is 30 minutes of audio", and also "at least an hour" and "closer to 2-3 hours". Split hours of audio into files of about 30 minutes.
- [ ] MP3 at 192kbps or above ("We strongly recommend MP3 at 192kbps or above"). WAV "usually do[es] not improve clone quality" and can cause upload problems.
- [ ] Keep the original sample files. "You cannot download or export your voice clones as standalone files." To make the clone again, you need the samples ("Each clone will sound slightly different, even when you use the same audio").

## Plans (checked 2026-11-26, regular prices; sale prices on the page end December 1)
- [ ] Instant Voice Clone: Starter or above ("Cloning is only available on the Starter tier and above"). Starter was $6 a month when checked.
- [ ] Professional Voice Clone: Creator or above. Creator was $22 a month when checked. Free and Starter have no Professional clone slots.
- [ ] Paid plans: "When generating content on our paid plans, you get commercial rights to use that content."
- [ ] Sources: elevenlabs.io/pricing ; elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/administration/billing

## Making the clone (ElevenLabs' docs)
- [ ] Instant: Voices on the left, the plus icon, Instant Voice Clone. Upload or record. Name and label it, "confirm that you have the right and consent to clone the voice", then Save voice. Then My Voices, Use voice.
- [ ] Professional: the plus next to Voices, then Create new under Professional Voice Clone. Upload samples, or Record yourself (sample scripts for narration, conversation and adverts). Audio settings next to a clip removes background noise or separates speakers.
- [ ] The voice check: the same or similar microphone and tone as the samples; say each line once, then press Stop. If it fails, wait 24 hours and try again, or contact support.
- [ ] Training: usually 3-6 hours, up to 24 hours; an email when it's ready.
- [ ] On Eleven v4, the docs list two voice settings, Stability and Similarity, and say "Style and Speed sliders are not available in Eleven v4". A Professional clone on Eleven v4 adds Clone Strength: "We recommend 0% for most use cases", about 50% for unique accents or traits.

## Your voice and ElevenLabs' terms (Terms of Service, non-EEA version, last updated 09 October 2026)
- [ ] I've checked the licence the terms give ElevenLabs over my voice. In their words, it lets ElevenLabs use your voice "to provide and improve the Services, and to develop new services and products"; "we will not commercialize your voice on a standalone basis without your permission to do so"; and the licence is "perpetual and irrevocable (which means this license cannot be withdrawn)". Source: elevenlabs.io/terms-of-use (the page has a separate version for the EEA, Switzerland and the UK)

## YouTube (checked 2026-11-26)
- [ ] YouTube lists "Cloning one's own voice to create voice overs or dubs" among examples creators don't need to disclose.
- [ ] It also lists, as something creators must disclose, content that "Makes a real person appear to say or do something they didn't do."
- [ ] "The above list is not exhaustive." Check it for your own video: support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491
