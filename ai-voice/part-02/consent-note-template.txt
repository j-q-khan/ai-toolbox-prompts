NOT LEGAL ADVICE. A plain note for when another adult agrees to let you clone their voice.
For an ElevenLabs Instant Voice Clone only: its Professional Voice Clone page says "Even with their consent, you cannot clone someone else's voice."
ElevenLabs' use policy bans cloning a living person's voice "without their explicit and informed consent or other valid authorization", and says "You may not submit voice recordings of anyone under 18 to create or train a voice model."
