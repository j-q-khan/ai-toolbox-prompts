Title: Planting tomato seedlings

Script:
Welcome to the garden shed. Today we're planting three tomato seedlings, and it takes about twenty minutes from start to finish.
First, fill each pot with fresh compost, and press it down gently with two fingers, so the roots have something firm to grow into.
Next, make a hole about as deep as your thumb, drop the seedling in, and firm the compost around the stem.
Water each pot until it drips from the bottom, then put them somewhere bright and warm, like a sunny windowsill.
In about two weeks, you'll see the first new leaves, and that's when they're ready for a bigger pot.
