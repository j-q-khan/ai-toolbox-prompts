Goal: build a small Python script, storyboard.py, that turns a plain text file of steps into a storyboard for a short animated explainer, one scene per step, with a title scene, the seconds each scene gets, and a ready-to-paste request for Claude Motion.
Context: Claude Motion (beta, Team and Enterprise plans) builds an animation from a prompt. Anthropic's help page says to say who will watch it, where it will play and roughly how long it should be, and to attach the report, data or images you want it to use. Motion doesn't use a video generation model, so it doesn't create realistic footage or people. The storyboard must only use words, numbers, simple charts and shapes, never realistic people or scenes. Use made-up steps only.
Steps, in order:
1. Tell me your plan in a few lines and wait for my OK. Don't create, change or delete any file before I say yes.
2. Create sample-steps.txt with made-up onboarding steps for "Test Bakery": a first line "Title: Your first day, in three steps", a line "Audience: new starters", a line "Plays at: their first morning", a line "Length: 30", then three steps, one per line, starting with a dash: "- Get your login from the front desk", "- Find your buddy, who shows you round", "- Your first shift, with your buddy beside you". Use no real names, companies or emails.
3. Write storyboard.py with the Python standard library only. It takes the steps file's path and an output path as arguments. It reads the Title, Audience, Plays at and Length lines (Length is the total in seconds; default 30 when missing) and every line that starts with a dash as a step.
4. Give the title scene 4 seconds and a closing scene 4 seconds, and share the rest evenly between the steps, rounded to whole seconds so the total still equals Length. If Length leaves fewer than 3 seconds a step, stop and say so instead of writing anything.
5. Write a Markdown storyboard: a table with the columns Scene, Seconds, On screen and Motion idea, where On screen is the step's words and Motion idea is a plain suggestion such as "the step card slides in" or "the number counts up". Never suggest realistic people, faces or filmed scenes.
6. Under the table, write a heading "Request for Claude Motion" and one paragraph that says who will watch, where it will play, how long it is, and the scenes in order, ready to paste into a chat after choosing Output, then Motion.
7. If the file has no steps, print a clear message and write nothing. Never overwrite an existing output file: stop and tell me.
8. Run it on sample-steps.txt only, writing storyboard.md, and show me storyboard.md.
9. Add a short README.md: how to run it on Windows and Mac, that it never sends anything anywhere, that Claude Motion needs a Team or Enterprise plan with Motion turned on, and a reminder to remove private details from real material and to follow your organization's rules before using it.
Safety: work only inside this folder. No network access, no installs, no other packages. Don't open or upload real company files. Ask before every file you create.
