Context: Claude Whiteboards (beta, on Pro, Max, Ultra, Team and Enterprise plans) turns a brain dump into a board, and Anthropic's help page says "Claude works from what's on the board and doesn't use context from your connected apps", so the dates and details have to be in what you give it. Use made-up notes only.
