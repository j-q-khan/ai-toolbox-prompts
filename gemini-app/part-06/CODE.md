# Gemini Canvas: starter prompts and checklist (facts checked 2026-11-18)

To keep a copy: paste this into Notepad and save it with the box called Save as type set to All files. On a Mac, open TextEdit, choose Format, then Make Plain Text, before you paste and save.
Written from Google's "Create docs, apps & more with Canvas" help page, for personal Google accounts, on a computer at gemini.google.com.
Google's tip: "To have Gemini use a specific format or document type, like a slide deck, include this instruction in your prompt."
Swap the [brackets] for your own words. Use made-up names, never real people's details.

## Before you start
- [ ] Signed in (Google: "To use this feature, you must be signed in to Gemini Apps.") Sign in is at the top right.
- [ ] Below the text box at the bottom: Add Files, then Canvas. (Phone app: Add, then Canvas.)
- [ ] Type one prompt below, then Submit.

## 1. A doc
Create a document: a one-page welcome guide for new staff at [Test Bakery, a made-up shop]. Include opening hours, who to ask for help, and a first-day checklist. Keep it friendly and plain.

## 2. Slides
Create a slide deck: five slides about [how our weekend market works]. A title slide, three slides with three short lines each, and a last slide for questions. Plain words, no jargon.

## 3. Doc to slides (after the doc is made; 18 or over)
At the top right of the Canvas panel, click Create, then type:
Turn this into five slides: a title slide, one slide per section, and a last slide for questions. Keep each slide to three short lines.

## 4. A quiz
Create a quiz to test me on [the water cycle], for a beginner. Ten multiple-choice questions, and after each answer, a one-line explanation.

## 5. A simple app
Create an app: a tip calculator. Boxes for the bill amount, the tip percentage and the number of people, and it shows what each person pays. Big buttons, plain labels, no sign-up.

## Change one part
- [ ] Select the text in the Canvas panel, type the change in the text box, press Enter. Example: Make this paragraph friendlier and shorter.
- [ ] Or select it and click Suggest edits.
- [ ] Change length or Change tone (in the Canvas panel) to make the text shorter or longer, more casual or more formal.
- [ ] Previous Version / Next Version (at the top) to look at saved versions.

## Get it out (on a computer)
- [ ] Share & export (top right of the Canvas panel), then Export to Docs (a new Google Doc), Export to Slides (Google Slides, or a PDF), or Copy contents.
- [ ] Phone app: More (top of the Canvas panel), then Export to Docs, or Select text, then Copy.
- [ ] Sharing a link: Google says shared links open only at gemini.google.com, not in the Gemini mobile app.
- [ ] Sharing an app: Google says anyone with that public link can view and edit data associated with the app.

## If Canvas doesn't show up
- [ ] Signed in? (top right)
- [ ] Work account: a qualifying Workspace edition and 18 or over. School account: your administrator must turn on access to Gemini.
- [ ] Under 18: Create (doc to slides, quiz, web page) and adding Gemini features to an app aren't available yet.
- [ ] Skills don't work with Canvas yet.

## Limits (Google's words)
- Canvas is listed for no plan, Google AI Plus, Pro and Ultra.
- "Your limit refreshes every 5 hours until you reach your weekly limit."
- "Start a new chat when you move to a new topic."

Sources: support.google.com/gemini/answer/16047321 ; support.google.com/gemini/answer/16275805 ; support.google.com/gemini/answer/13278668 ; support.google.com/gemini/answer/18560919
