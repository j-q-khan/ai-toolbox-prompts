# Gemini Live: first chats to try, and a screen-sharing safety checklist (facts checked 2026-11-19)

To keep a copy: paste this into Notepad and save it with the box called Save as type set to All files. On a Mac, open TextEdit, choose Format, then Make Plain Text, before you paste and save.
Written from Google's "Talk naturally with Gemini Live" help page and the Gemini Apps Privacy Hub, for the Gemini mobile app on Android and iPhone.
Google: "For now, Gemini Live isn't available in the Gemini web app."

## Before you start
- [ ] The Gemini mobile app on an Android phone or tablet, or an iPhone or iPad.
- [ ] Signed in, with a personal Google account, or a work or school account with access to Gemini Apps.
- [ ] Age: Google's list says "Be over 13 (or the applicable age in your country)." and "Be 18 or over." Check the page if you're under 18.
- [ ] Start: at the bottom of the app, tap Live, or swipe left. First time: follow any on-screen instructions.

## First chats to try (voice only)
1. Help me plan a birthday dinner for six people on a small budget. Ask me questions one at a time.
2. I have a two-minute talk tomorrow. Let me practise it out loud, then tell me one thing to improve.
3. Quiz me on the capitals of Europe, one at a time, and tell me if I'm right.

## First chats to try (camera on: tap Camera)
4. What plant is this, and how often should I water it?
5. Here's what's in my fridge. What could I cook tonight in 30 minutes?
6. Which of these two cables goes into the back of my router?
(Switch to rear or front camera flips the view. Tap Camera again to stop.)

## First chats to try (screen sharing on: tap Turn on screen sharing)
7. I'm on a settings page and can't find where to change my text size. Walk me through it one step at a time.
8. Explain what this error message on my screen means, in plain words.

## Screen-sharing safety checklist
- [ ] Notifications for Gemini Live are on (Google: required to share your screen). Android: Settings app, Notifications, App notifications, Google, then All Google Notifications and Live with Gemini.
- [ ] Remember Google's words: "When you share your screen, your full screen is shared with Gemini."
- [ ] Google: "Don't enter confidential information that you wouldn't want a reviewer to see"
- [ ] Our advice: put away banking apps, private messages, passwords, codes and other people's details before you share.
- [ ] Google: "ask permission before you record or include them in a Live chat"
- [ ] Muted is not stopped: on Android, Google says your video or screen continues to be shared while muted.
- [ ] Stop: in the Gemini app, Turn off screen sharing; or swipe down from the top edge, then on the Screen Sharing card, Stop sharing.
- [ ] On hold or locked: screen sharing stops and doesn't restart. Share again if you need it.
- [ ] Finished: tap End (or swipe right) to end the chat and see the transcript.

## What's kept (Google's Privacy Hub)
- History on: recordings, transcripts and what you share with Gemini Live are stored in Gemini History.
- History off: Gemini Live chats are stored for around two months.
- "Your Gemini Live audio, video and screenshares are not used to improve Google services by default."

Sources: support.google.com/gemini/answer/15274899 ; support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961 ; gemini.google/us/subscriptions
