# Your first day with Gemini: checklist (facts checked 2026-11-18)

To keep a copy: paste this into Notepad and save it with the box called Save as type set to All files. On a Mac, open TextEdit, choose Format, then Make Plain Text, before you paste and save.

## Before you start
- [ ] A computer with a web browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Opera or Edge). Same steps on Windows and Mac.
- [ ] A Google account. Personal accounts: 13 or over, or the age your country sets. Many features need 18 or over.
- [ ] For work: follow your employer's rules first.

## Your first prompt
- [ ] Go to gemini.google.com and sign in.
- [ ] Type prompt 1 from first-prompts.txt in the box at the bottom, then click Submit.
- [ ] Reply in the same chat with prompt 2 to change the answer.

## The model picker
- [ ] Click the model's name at the bottom of the chat box to see the models you can choose. Leave auto model selection on for now (at the bottom left: Settings, then Usage limits).
- [ ] A lock icon means a higher plan is needed.
- [ ] Usage limits refresh every 5 hours until you reach your weekly limit (Google's words). No fixed numbers are published.

## Files
- [ ] Below the text box: Add files, then Upload files. Use a test file, not a private one.
- [ ] Up to 10 files a prompt; most files up to 100 MB, videos up to 2 GB.

## Check the answer
- [ ] Click Sources at the bottom of an answer and open one link. Does the page say the same thing?
- [ ] No Sources button? Ask where the information came from, or look it up yourself.

## Privacy
- [ ] History on: Google stores your Gemini chats in your Google account. On by default if you're 18 or over.
- [ ] To turn it off: click History, then near the top click On, then Turn off.
- [ ] One-off question: click Temporary chat, next to New chat. Temporary chats are not used to train Google's AI models.
- [ ] Don't enter confidential information that you wouldn't want a reviewer to see.

## When an answer misses
- [ ] Below the answer, click Regenerate, then use the arrows to switch versions. Latest answer only.

Sources: support.google.com/gemini/answer/13275745 ; support.google.com/gemini/answer/13278668 ; support.google.com/googleone/answer/18648722 ; support.google.com/gemini/answer/16275805 ; support.google.com/gemini/answer/14903178 ; support.google.com/gemini/answer/14143489 ; support.google.com/gemini/answer/14262426 ; support.google.com/gemini/answer/13278892 ; support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961
