# Gemini app: what each device needs (facts checked 2026-11-18)

To keep a copy: paste this into Notepad and save it with the box called Save as type set to All files. On a Mac, open TextEdit, choose Format, then Make Plain Text, before you paste and save.
Written from Google's Gemini help pages, Apple's and Microsoft's help pages. Requirements change: re-check the pages before you buy anything.

## Any device: your account
- [ ] A Google account. Personal: 13 or over (or the age in your country). Work: a qualifying Workspace edition, and 18 or over. School: turned on by your institution's administrator.
- [ ] Some features may not be available under 18 (Google's limits page).
- [ ] Same Google account on every device, so your chat history syncs (Google's desktop page).

## Windows app
- [ ] Windows 10 or later (Google's desktop page: x64 and ARM64).
- [ ] 8 GB of RAM or more. Check: right-click Start, Settings, System, About, then Device specifications (installed RAM).
- [ ] At least 200 MB of free disk space, and a stable internet connection.
- [ ] Download: gemini.google/desktop, click Download for Windows, save GeminiSetup.exe, open it, follow the instructions on screen.
- [ ] Open: Start menu, double-click Gemini, sign in. Shortcut: Alt + Space.
- [ ] Skills and Tasks: Google's pages list the web, Mac and mobile apps, not Windows (checked 2026-11-18). Use gemini.google.com in your browser for those.

## Mac app
- [ ] Apple silicon. Check: Apple menu, About This Mac. "Chip" means Apple silicon; "Processor" with an Intel name means an Intel Mac, which isn't on Google's list.
- [ ] macOS Sequoia (15.0) or later.
- [ ] 8 GB of RAM or more, and at least 200 MB of free disk space.
- [ ] Download: gemini.google/desktop, click Download for macOS, open the .dmg file in Downloads, drag Gemini into Applications.
- [ ] Open: Applications, double-click Gemini, sign in if asked. Option + Space for mini chat, Option + Shift + Space for the full chat.
- [ ] Extras: both Command keys share your screen; hold fn to talk into any app (Speak to Window, rolling out).
- [ ] Updates: Gemini, then Check for Updates, in the top menu bar.

## iPhone and iPad
- [ ] The Google Gemini app (Google LLC) in the App Store.
- [ ] Google's help page says iOS 16 and up; the App Store listing said "Requires iOS 18.2 or later" on 2026-11-18. Go by the listing.

## Android
- [ ] Some phones already have Gemini as the assistant. Otherwise: the Gemini app from the Google Play Store.
- [ ] Android 9 or later, 2 GB of RAM or more. Not on Android Go, and not in an Android Work Profile.
- [ ] Switching makes Gemini replace Google Assistant as your phone's main assistant.

## Check it worked
- [ ] Computer: press your shortcut (Alt + Space or Option + Space); Gemini comes up over your work, with your chats from the browser.
- [ ] Phone: open the app, make sure you're signed in, then type a question or tap the microphone.
- [ ] Shortcut clashes with another app? Change it in the Gemini app's settings (on a Mac: Gemini, then Settings, in the top menu bar).

## No app for your device?
- [ ] Use gemini.google.com in Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Opera or Edge.
