# Gemini app: which model for which job (facts checked 2026-11-19)

To keep a copy: paste this into Notepad and save it with the box called Save as type set to All files. On a Mac, open TextEdit, choose Format, then Make Plain Text, before you paste and save.
Written from Google's "About Gemini models" help page, for personal Google accounts. Work and school accounts: Google says models depend on your organization's Workspace license. Google changed this list on 2026-11-18 and says model availability "is subject to change without notice": re-check the page.

## The models, in Google's words
- Gemini 4 Hydrogen: "A high-speed, capable model that balances performance and responsiveness across standard chat and task modes."
- Gemini 4 Neon: "Frontier-level intelligence, with high performance for deeper analysis and complex workflows."
- Gemini 4 Carbon: "Our strongest model designed for advanced coding and your hardest, long-horizon problems"
- Auto: "Gemini will select Hydrogen or a fast, lightweight model, as well as the appropriate thinking level for each prompt, while conserving your usage."

## Which plan lists which (Google's table, chats)
- No plan: Auto.
- Google AI Plus: Auto, Hydrogen.
- Google AI Pro: Auto, Hydrogen, Neon.
- Google AI Ultra: Auto, Hydrogen, Neon, Carbon.
- Tasks: Hydrogen (no plan, Plus); Hydrogen, Neon (Pro); Hydrogen, Neon, Carbon (Ultra).
- Thinking levels, when you pick a model yourself: Low, Medium, High. Not chosen = Low.
- Context window (limits page): no plan 32k tokens; Plus 128k; Pro 1 million; Ultra 1 million.
- Google AI Pro was $19.99 a month (US) when checked on 2026-11-19.

## My suggestions (not Google's ranking; from the descriptions above)
- [ ] No plan: Auto is what you have.
- [ ] Plus: Auto for everyday chats; Hydrogen on Medium or High for tricky questions.
- [ ] Pro: Neon on High for complex work (long reports, deeper analysis).
- [ ] Ultra: Carbon for advanced coding and your hardest jobs with many steps.
- [ ] Auto won't pick Neon or Carbon for you. Google's tip: "To use more advanced models, select them manually."
- [ ] Small job (a dinner idea): Auto. Big job (every date and cost in a 20-page lease): Neon on High.

## Usage (Google's words)
- "More advanced models and higher thinking levels require more of your usage."
- "If you have a Google AI plan and reach your usage limit, your chats will continue with our fast, lightweight model."
- "Your limit refreshes every 5 hours until you reach your weekly limit."
- See your limits: Settings, then Usage limits (bottom left of gemini.google.com). Gemini tells you when you're getting near a limit.

## How to
- [ ] Switch model (paid plans): at the bottom of the chat box, click (computer) or tap (phone app) the model's name, then the model you want; optionally Low, Medium or High. A lock icon means a higher plan. The choice stays for that chat.
- [ ] Check what a chat is set to: the model's name at the bottom of the chat box. Google's pages don't say how to see which model Auto used for one answer (checked 2026-11-19).

## Names you may still see, not on Google's models page on 2026-11-19
- Flash-Lite, Gemini Pro, Gemini 4 Argon (listed on 2026-11-18).
- Gemini 3 Pro, Gemini 3 Flash ("Fast", "Thinking", "Pro"), 3.1 Pro, 3.5 Flash, 3.6 Flash, 3.8 Flash.

Sources: support.google.com/googleone/answer/18648722 ; support.google.com/gemini/answer/16275805 ; support.google.com/gemini/answer/14517446 ; gemini.google/us/subscriptions ; gemini.google/release-notes ; blog.google (3.8 Flash, Sep 02, 2026)
