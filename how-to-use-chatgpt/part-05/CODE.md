# ChatGPT app: safe-download checklist
# From How to Use ChatGPT, Part 5. Facts checked 2026-11-22 from OpenAI's help pages and the official store listings. Written from the docs, not run.

# How to use it: tick each line as you go. Requirements change: the store listing is the final word.

PHONE
[ ] iPhone or iPad: open the App Store and search for "OpenAI ChatGPT".
[ ] App Store: check that the developer is OpenAI OpCo, LLC.
[ ] Android: open Google Play and search for "OpenAI ChatGPT".
[ ] Google Play: check that the publisher is OpenAI.
[ ] Any other name? Don't install it. Go back and search again.
[ ] iPhone: the App Store listing says "Requires iOS 18.0 or later." (checked 2026-11-22)
[ ] Android: check the Google Play listing for the latest requirements.
[ ] Install, open, and sign in to the same ChatGPT account you use everywhere else.

MAC
[ ] Go to https://chatgpt.com/download (OpenAI's own download page).
[ ] Needs macOS 14 or newer, with Apple Silicon (M1 or better) or an Intel processor.
[ ] OpenAI's Mac help page says the download page detects Apple Silicon or Intel and gives you the right installer.
[ ] Intel Mac: use chatgpt.com/download, not the "Download for macOS (Apple Silicon)" button in OpenAI's desktop guide.

WINDOWS
[ ] Get it from the Microsoft Store. OpenAI's desktop guide links to the listing called "ChatGPT".
[ ] Check that the publisher is OpenAI.
[ ] The store listing says "Windows 10 version 19041.0 or higher". OpenAI's Windows help page says 17763.0 and still calls the app "an early version". Go by the store listing.

LINUX
[ ] Preview only. Follow OpenAI's guide: https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/linux/linux-app

AFTER YOU SIGN IN
[ ] Open the app and sign in with your ChatGPT account.
[ ] Had the older desktop app? It's now ChatGPT Classic. It can stay installed alongside the new app and still gets updates.
[ ] Stay on Chat: pick ChatGPT from the menu, then Chat on the switch above the box where you type.
[ ] Quick chat (ChatGPT only): Mac ⌘+⌥+N, Windows Ctrl+Alt+N.
[ ] Every shortcut: Mac ⌘+/, Windows Ctrl+/. Change them in Settings, then Keyboard Shortcuts.
[ ] Appshot: both Command keys (Mac) or both Alt keys (Windows). It can include text scrolled out of view, so keep private windows out of it.
[ ] Phone, two accounts (rolling out): open the sidebar, select your profile icon to open Settings, select Switch account, then Add account, and sign in (up to 2 accounts). No Switch account yet? It may not be available to you yet.

MONEY AND ACCOUNTS
[ ] Paid, but the app shows Free? Sign in to the same ChatGPT account that made the purchase before you subscribe again.
[ ] Uninstalling the app does not cancel your subscription. To cancel: https://help.openai.com/articles/7232927
[ ] Store listings still mention "Advanced Voice Mode", which OpenAI says it is retiring from ChatGPT Voice on December 11, 2026. Go by the app and help.openai.com.
