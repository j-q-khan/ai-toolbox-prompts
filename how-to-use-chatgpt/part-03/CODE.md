```
# ChatGPT Images: a prompt pack for making and editing pictures
# From How to Use ChatGPT, Part 3. Facts checked 2026-11-21 from OpenAI's help pages. Written from the docs, not run.
# How to save this file:
#   Windows: open Notepad, paste this text, choose File, Save as, set the box called Save as type to All files,
#   and name it chatgpt-image-prompts.txt
#   Mac: open TextEdit, choose Format, Make Plain Text, paste this text, and save it as chatgpt-image-prompts.txt
# How to use a prompt: copy the text after "PROMPT:" into the message box on chatgpt.com or in the phone app.
# Change the words in [square brackets] to your own. Every example here is made up.

CHECKLIST (OpenAI: "Be specific about the details that matter.")
- What's in it: the main thing in the picture
- Style: watercolour, flat poster, pencil sketch, photo-style
- Colours: the main colours you want
- Words: the exact words, if any, and where they go
- Shape (aspect ratio): tall like a phone screen, wide like a television, or square

MAKE A FIRST PICTURE
PROMPT: Make a picture of a red paper boat on a calm pond at sunset, in soft watercolour.
PROMPT: Make a picture of [subject] in [place], in a [style] style, with [main colours], [tall / wide / square].

MAKE A POSTER WITH WORDS ON IT
PROMPT: A poster for a made-up school bake sale: cupcakes on a striped tablecloth, bright and cheerful, the words "Bake Sale Saturday" across the top, tall like a phone screen.
PROMPT: A poster for [event]: [what is shown], [mood], the words "[your exact words]" across the top, [tall / wide / square].

CHANGE ONE PART (website: select the picture, choose Select, highlight the area, then describe the change)
PROMPT: Make the tablecloth plain blue and keep everything else the same.
PROMPT: Change only the [area you highlighted] to [what you want], and keep everything else the same.
# OpenAI: "Highlights are not always precise, and edits may extend beyond the area you selected."
# OpenAI: "If you want the edit to apply to a specific area, include that area in your prompt."
# Spilled over? Ask again and name the area in your prompt. While you're highlighting, Undo takes back a highlight and Cancel starts over.

CHANGE THE SHAPE
PROMPT: Make this same picture wide like a television, and keep everything else the same.
# Or use the aspect ratio picker, or the Aspect ratio button in the editor.

REUSE A SAVED PHOTO (upload a photo, ask ChatGPT to save it as a reference, and give it a name)
PROMPT: Save this photo as a reference called [name].
PROMPT: Make a watercolour portrait of @[name].

RULES (from OpenAI's help page, Terms of Use and usage policies, checked 2026-11-21)
- Every picture you create is saved under Images. To delete one, delete the conversation where it was made.
- Images made with ChatGPT include a C2PA label and a SynthID watermark. A screenshot or a converted file can lose the label; keep the downloaded original.
- As between you and OpenAI, and as far as the law allows, you own the output, but it may not be unique.
- Don't use someone's photorealistic likeness without their consent in ways that could confuse authenticity.
- Don't pass off a picture as made by a person when it wasn't.
- Our habit: never share a realistic picture of a person or an event as if it really happened.
- Limits depend on your plan and are described in words, not numbers: check chatgpt.com/pricing.
```
