CHECKLIST
[ ] 1. Open chatgpt.com in a browser, or the ChatGPT app for iPhone or Android. Type in the message box and send.
[ ] 2. Answer missed? Reply in the same chat ("Make it shorter and warmer.") instead of starting over.
[ ] 3. Want plain answers? Website: Settings, then Personalization, then Layout and Visuals, then Simple.
[ ] 4. Model menu: pick GPT-6, then set the Thinking slider (Instant, Medium, High, Extra High, Pro). Plus gets Medium and High. Free and Go get a lighter model and no slider.
[ ] 5. Add a file: the + where you type, then "Add photos & files". Free: 3 uploads a day. Nothing private.
[ ] 6. Search the web: View all tools, then Search (or type / and pick Search). Open the linked pages to check them.
[ ] 7. Memory: Settings, then Personalization, then Memory. Deleting a chat doesn't necessarily delete a memory made from it.
[ ] 8. Custom instructions: Settings, then Personalization, Enable customization on, then Custom Instructions. Phone: Settings, then Customize ChatGPT. Free and Go: up to 1,500 characters.
[ ] 9. Projects (signed in): sidebar, then New project (phone: the + icon). Project instructions: the three-dot menu, then Project settings.
[ ] 10. Temporary chat: open a new chat, select Temporary, choose Personalized or Unpersonalized before your first message.
[ ] 11. Training switch: account menu, then Settings, then Data controls, then "Improve the model for everyone". Phone: profile icon, then Settings, then Data controls. A thumbs up or down can still send that chat for training.
[ ] 12. Keep passwords, bank details and other people's private information out of your prompts. At work, follow your employer's rules.

FIRST PROMPTS (swap every [square bracket] for your own made-up or general details)
1. Write a short, friendly note telling my [book club] we're meeting on [Thursday] instead.
2. Make it shorter and warmer.
3. What are the three main points in this [price list]? Then tell me the one thing I need to do.
4. Search the web first, then tell me [something recent, like a shop's opening hours], and show me the pages you used.
5. Ask me three short questions about what I want help with this week, one at a time, then suggest three prompts I could try.
