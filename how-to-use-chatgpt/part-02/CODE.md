```
# ChatGPT model menu: which Thinking level for which job
# From How to Use ChatGPT, Part 2. Facts checked 2026-11-21 from OpenAI's help pages. Written from the docs, not run.
# How to save this file:
#   Windows: open Notepad, paste this text, choose File, Save as, set the box called Save as type to All files,
#   and name it chatgpt-thinking-levels.txt
#   Mac: open TextEdit, choose Format, Make Plain Text, paste this text, and save it as chatgpt-thinking-levels.txt
# This is about regular Chat. GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol are picked in Work and Codex, not Chat.
# The "Try it for" column is our suggestion; the "What OpenAI says it's for" column is OpenAI's own wording.
# If your menu looks different, check help.openai.com: model names and plan levels change often.

LEVELS (Level | What OpenAI says it's for | Engine | Try it for)
Instant | Fast responses for everyday questions. | GPT-6 Sol on paid plans | rewording an email, a quick fact (our suggestion)
Medium | Standard thinking. | GPT-6 Sol | weighing up two options (our suggestion)
High | Extended thinking. | GPT-6 Sol | a plan with several parts, a long document (our suggestion)
Extra High | The highest thinking level available with GPT-6 Sol. | GPT-6 Sol | the hardest jobs you'd give a careful expert (our suggestion)
Pro | Pro models for difficult tasks and longer-running workflows. | GPT-6 Pro, powered by GPT-6 Astra | difficult jobs with many steps; slower, with a weekly limit (our suggestion)

PLANS IN CHAT (Plan | What you get in the model menu)
Free | None of the levels above Instant. Everyday questions get the default model; Think for harder questions (on a phone: the + menu, then Think). Both run on Luna; OpenAI's pages disagree on the version.
Go | None of the levels above Instant. Everyday questions get the default model; Think for harder questions. Both run on Luna; OpenAI's pages disagree on the version.
Plus | Of the levels above Instant: Medium and High only. No Extra High, no Pro level, so no Astra in Chat (Plus gets GPT-6 Astra in Work and Codex).
Pro plan | Every level, including the Pro level (GPT-6 Astra).
Business | Every level, including the Pro level.
Enterprise | Every level, including the Pro level.

NOTES
- "A Pro subscription does not include unlimited use of the GPT-6 Pro model." Past the weekly limit you are switched to an older model at Medium, or you pick another model or wait for the reset, depending on your Pro plan.
- The Pro level (GPT-6 Astra) does not support Intelligent UI, so answers there come back without charts and buttons.
- If you picked GPT-5.6 Sol yourself before GPT-6 arrived, you are still on it: open the model menu and check.
- GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol (November 17, 2026) are in ChatGPT Work and Codex, on Plus and above, not in regular Chat.
```
