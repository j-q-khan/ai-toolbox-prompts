# Photoshop 28 AI tools: checklist and prompt sheet (facts checked 2026-11-25)

To keep a copy: paste this into Notepad and save it with the box called Save as type set to All files, as photoshop-ai-checklist.md. On a Mac: open TextEdit, choose Format, then Make Plain Text, before you paste and save.

Every step below is from Adobe's own pages on 2026-11-25. Nothing was signed up for, installed, run, edited or generated. Menus and credit costs change: check the links. Try every edit on a copy of your photo first.

## Before you start
- [ ] Ask before you edit a photo of someone, and never pass an edited photo off as the real moment. Adobe's guidelines say don't "Violate others' privacy rights" or go "Impersonating other people or entities".
- [ ] Know your credits: the Photoshop plan includes 25 generative credits a month (subscribed on or after June 17, 2025). Credits do not roll over. Check your balance at https://account.adobe.com

## What each tool costs (Adobe's generative credits FAQ)
- [ ] Generative Fill with Adobe Firefly models: 1 credit per generation.
- [ ] Generative Fill with partner models: no fixed number given ("Learn more about partner models").
- [ ] Remove tool: no credits. Remove related elements: 10 credits per use.
- [ ] Relight: 30 credits per generation.

## Generative Fill (add or replace something)
- [ ] Select the area with the Selection Brush tool, or any other selection tool.
- [ ] Click the Generative Fill button in the Contextual Task Bar.
- [ ] Type a prompt describing what you want, or leave it blank to fill from the surroundings.
- [ ] Click Generate.
- [ ] Your edit lands on a new Generative Layer in the Layers panel, so the original stays editable.

## Remove tool (take things out)
- [ ] Select the Remove tool from the Tools panel, then Find distractions from the Options bar.
- [ ] Choose Wires and cables, People, or General distractions.
- [ ] Review what it marked, and brush to refine it.
- [ ] Select the checkmark icon to commit the change.
- [ ] Photoshop 28.0: Remove related elements also removes shadows, reflections, ripples and other related effects (10 credits per use).

## Relight (change the light, new in 28.0)
- [ ] Adobe's release notes: "Add, adjust, and reshape light in any photo. Change direction, intensity, warmth, and softness, or introduce entirely new light sources, all without re-shooting."
- [ ] Up to three light sources, adjusted on the image canvas.
- [ ] It costs 30 credits per generation: more than the Photoshop plan's 25 a month.

## Generative Fill greyed out? Check in this order
- [ ] There is an active selection on the image.
- [ ] The file is in RGB mode.
- [ ] The file is 8-bit (not 16- or 32-bit). Adobe's examples of the right format: JPEG or PSD.
- [ ] You are on an unlocked raster layer: not a text layer, a smart object (unless rasterized) or a vector shape.
- [ ] You are signed in at https://account.adobe.com with a valid Adobe ID.
- [ ] You have generative credits left: "Generative Fill is unavailable without generative credits."
- [ ] Photoshop is up to date (Generative Fill needs version 25.0 or later; check under Help, then System Info).
- [ ] You are connected to the internet, and no firewall or VPN is blocking Adobe's AI services.
- [ ] You are not on an Enterprise or Education account with restricted AI access.
- [ ] Your country is supported: not available in China, Russia, Belarus, Cuba, Iran, North Korea or Syria.
- [ ] Still stuck: Preferences, then General, then Reset Preferences on Quit, and restart Photoshop.

## Before you share
- [ ] Window, then Content Credentials (Beta), then Enable Content Credentials, to attach attribution information and editing history when you export.
- [ ] Photoshop 28.0: compare your edit with the original using Quick Compare (split-screen or single view).
