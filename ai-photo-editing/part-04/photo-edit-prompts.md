# Edit your own photos with a prompt: ChatGPT and Gemini (checked 2026-11-25)

Consent rule: edit only your own photos, with nobody else in them. Never edit a photo of someone else, and never pass an edited photo off as real. OpenAI's usage policies ban "use of someone's likeness, including their photorealistic image or voice, without their consent in ways that could confuse authenticity". Google's Gemini help page says: "Be sure not to violate others' copyright or privacy rights."

To keep a copy: paste this into Notepad and save it with the box called Save as type set to All files, as photo-edit-prompts.md. On a Mac: open TextEdit, choose Format, then Make Plain Text, before you paste and save.

Every step below is from the maker's own help page on 2026-11-25. Nothing was uploaded, edited or generated. Apps change: check each link. Swap the words in square brackets for your own photo.

## Remove something
- Remove the bin from the sand and fill the space with more beach. Keep everything else in the photo as it is.
- Remove the sign on the left of the path and fill the space with the hedge behind it.
- Remove the cable across the top of the photo and fill the space with sky.
- Remove the [thing] on the [left / right / top / bottom] of the photo and fill the space with [what is behind it].

## Change one part
- Make only the sky a clearer blue, and keep the sand, the sea and the edges as they are.
- Replace the plastic chair on the right with a wooden bench. Keep the rest of the photo the same.
- Change the colour of the front door to dark green, and change nothing else.

## Light and colour
- Make the whole photo a little brighter, without changing what is in it.
- Warm up the colours slightly, like late afternoon light, and keep every detail where it is.

## Ask again
- Keep the [thing] on the [left / right] exactly as it was in my original photo.
- That changed the [part]. Undo that part and keep only the change to the [thing].

## How to use it in ChatGPT
- Go to chatgpt.com. Select + in the prompt area, then Add photos & files, and pick your photo (or drag the image file into the text area).
- Describe the change, naming the area: "If you want the edit to apply to a specific area, include that area in your prompt."
- Or select the image in your conversation to open the editor, select Select, highlight the area, and describe the change. Undo or Redo revise your selections; Cancel starts over; Save when you are finished.
- Check the whole photo against your original: "Highlights are not always precise, and edits may extend beyond the area you selected."
- To download: open the image under Images, then select Save.
- File types: "PNG (.png), JPEG (.jpeg and .jpg), and non-animated GIF (.gif)", up to 20 MB each. "Free users are limited to 3 file uploads per day."

## How to use it in Gemini
- Sign in. "Editing images isn't available to users under 18."
- Go to gemini.google.com. Click Add files, then Upload Files, and pick your photo.
- Type the change in the text box at the bottom, saying where the thing is, then click Submit.
- To keep it: hover over the image and click Download full size.
- Check the whole photo against your original before you keep it.

## Not for
- Photos of other people, or of anyone's face.
- Removing a watermark, a label or anyone's name or mark from a picture.
- Making a brand-new picture from nothing: see the videos linked in the description.

## Hidden marks on what you make
- Gemini: "All AI-generated media created or edited with Gemini Apps includes an invisible SynthID watermark."
- ChatGPT: "Images generated with ChatGPT or the OpenAI API include both signals." (Content Credentials and SynthID)
- Leave them on when you share.
