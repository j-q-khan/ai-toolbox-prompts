# The AI eraser on your phone: checklist (facts checked 2026-11-25)

To keep a copy: paste this into Notepad and save it with the box called Save as type set to All files, as phone-eraser-checklist.md. On a Mac: open TextEdit, choose Format, then Make Plain Text, before you paste and save.

Every step below is from the maker's own help page on 2026-11-25. Nothing was signed up for, installed, run, edited or generated. Menus change: check the page for your phone. Try every edit on a copy of your photo first.

## Before you start
- [ ] Ask before you edit a photo of someone, and never pass an edited photo off as the real moment.
- [ ] Keep your original: save edits as a copy, or know how to undo them (below).

## Google Photos on Android
- [ ] Your phone: "To use most features, you must have at least 3 GB RAM and Android 8.0 and up." Some tools need 4 GB or 6 GB.
- [ ] Remove something: open the photo, tap Edit, open the Actions tab, tap Magic Eraser.
- [ ] Tap, draw a circle around, or brush the area you want to remove, then tap Erase.
- [ ] Save: tap Save as copy, which "Makes a new photo with your edits without changes to the original."
- [ ] Remember: "You can't undo or adjust changes to photos you save as a copy." For another go, start from the original.
- [ ] Saved over the original? Open it, tap Edit, then Revert.
- [ ] Blurry photo: Unblur is in the Actions tab. Improved Unblur needs at least 4 GB RAM.
- [ ] Cropped too tight: tap Edit, then Crop, then Auto frame, to recompose or extend the background.
- [ ] Check the label: open the photo, tap More, then About, and scroll to "How this was made". Content Credentials don't tell you if a photo is real or fake.
- [ ] Help page: https://support.google.com/photos/answer/6128850?co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid

## iPhone: Apple Photos (iOS 27)
- [ ] Your phone: iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, or iPhone 16 or later, with Apple Intelligence on iOS 27. Not in all languages or regions. Usage limits may apply.
- [ ] Remove something: open Photos, open the photo, tap Edit, tap Tools, tap Clean Up.
- [ ] Tap a highlighted item, or tap, brush or circle what you want to remove. Pinch with two fingers to zoom.
- [ ] Processing: tap Auto to choose Auto, Fast or High Quality. With High Quality, tap Clean Up to run it.
- [ ] Too tight: Edit, Tools, Extend. Pinch or drag to extend the background, then tap Extend.
- [ ] Wrong angle: Edit, Tools, Reframe. Drag to adjust the perspective, then tap Reframe. Tap the photo to compare with the original.
- [ ] Finish: tap Done, then Done again.
- [ ] Undo after saving: Edit, Tools, then Reset.
- [ ] iOS 27.2 or later: most images edited with Apple Intelligence tools include a SynthID hidden watermark.
- [ ] Guide: https://support.apple.com/en-gb/guide/iphone/xy0grrlxue5w/27/ios/27

## Samsung Gallery (Galaxy AI)
- [ ] Your phone: select phones and tablets running Android 14 with One UI 6.1 or higher. A Samsung account and internet connection are required.
- [ ] Open the Gallery app, pick the photo, tap Edit (the pencil), tap Photo assist (the blue circle with stars).
- [ ] Remove something: AI Eraser. Tap or outline it, then tap Erase. Tap Return to undo.
- [ ] Move something: Move. Drag it, rotate it or resize it, then tap Generate to fill the background.
- [ ] Save: tap Save as copy.
- [ ] Expect a Galaxy AI watermark on the edited image, and a photo resized to up to 12MP.
- [ ] Shortcut: open a photo, swipe up to see details, and tap an Edit suggestion if there is one.
- [ ] Help page: https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10000934/
