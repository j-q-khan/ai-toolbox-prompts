# System prompt and standing instructions: checklist (facts checked 2026-11-18)

For Claude or ChatGPT in a browser, on Windows or Mac. Nothing here was signed into or run for the video, no screen of either app was seen, and no result is promised. The Test Bakery lines are ours; the quoted lines are Anthropic's and OpenAI's, word for word, from the pages under Sources. Names and places change: if a step doesn't match what you see, open the page it came from.

## What it is
- [ ] A system prompt is the standing instructions an app gets before your message. Anthropic's release notes: Claude's apps "use a system prompt to provide up-to-date information, such as the current date, to Claude at the start of every conversation".
- [ ] Your version, on both companies' pages, is called instructions.

## Before you save anything
- [ ] Leave out anything about other people: names, phone numbers, emails, customer details. Leave out every password.
- [ ] Look at your app's own privacy page.
- [ ] At work: follow your employer's rules first.

## Write the lines (four to six short ones)
- [ ] Line 1, a role. Anthropic: "Setting a role in the system prompt focuses Claude's behavior and tone for your use case. Even a single sentence makes a difference".
- [ ] Who it's for, and the tone.
- [ ] The length, with its reason.
- [ ] What to do when a fact is missing.
- [ ] Only what holds every time. Today's task stays in the message.

## Where they go in Claude (Claude Help Center, checked 2026-11-18)
- [ ] For every chat: "Click your initials in the lower left corner." Then: "Select "Settings," then "Your Claude" (or "Account" in the older Settings layout)." Then: "Under "Instructions" (or "Instructions for Claude" in the older Settings layout), describe your instructions". The page: "Any instructions you add here will be applied to all of your conversations with Claude."
- [ ] For one Project: "Click on "Set project instructions."" Then: "Add instructions for how you'd like Claude to behave and respond and click "Save instructions."" The page: "These instructions only apply to chats within that project."

## Where they go in ChatGPT (OpenAI Academy, "Customizing ChatGPT", last updated October 6, 2026; checked 2026-11-18)
- [ ] The page: "You can set instructions at multiple levels: at the user level through settings, within a specific project's instructions, or even just in the chat itself when you need a one-off adjustment."
- [ ] For every chat: "Go to your profile and then click "Personalize."" The page gives no further step, so neither do we.
- [ ] For one project: "Set project instructions that define the style and tone for a specific body of work."
- [ ] A one-off change: say it in that chat, and leave your standing instructions alone.

## The files, and where each one goes
Open a file from the link in the description and copy all of it.
- [ ] 1-before.txt: ours. The line retyped at the top of every chat, to compare. Not to use.
- [ ] 2-standing-instructions.txt: ours. The four lines, set once, in one of the places above.
- [ ] 3-message.txt: ours. What's left to type: paste it into the message box at the bottom of a new chat.
- [ ] 4-template.txt: the blank version. Swap everything in square brackets, brackets included, for your own words.
- [ ] example-output.txt: not a prompt. It's the example reply shown in the video, which we wrote; nothing was run.
- [ ] BUILD-PROMPT.md: paste it into a new chat, put the lines you retype where the last line says so, and answer its questions.
